Source: Australian Transport Safety Bureau; AO-2016-102. Selected factual paraphrase of public final occurrence account, not an independent preliminary report.

Observation 1: On 17 August 2016 an Airbus A320 VH-VGI and Fokker F50 VH-FKV were involved in a runway conflict at Adelaide Airport.
Observation 2: The surface movement controller instructed the Fokker to hold short of runway 23, but the check captain read back a crossing instruction.
Observation 3: The Fokker entered runway 23 while the Airbus was approaching to land.
Observation 4: The surface movement controller instructed the Fokker to expedite; the Airbus had already begun going around when the aerodrome controller issued the go-around instruction at 0928:25.
Observation 5: The Fokker completed the crossing and the Airbus subsequently landed without further incident.
