Source: Australian Transport Safety Bureau; AO-2020-037. Selected factual paraphrase of public final occurrence account, not an independent preliminary report.

Observation 1: On 23 July 2020 a Sling 2 VH-ZSD and a Piper PA-28 VH-TAX were operating at Moorabbin Airport; five aircraft were in the 17L circuit.
Observation 2: The Sling was flown solo by a student pilot.
Observation 3: At 1334:49 EST the tower told the Sling to follow a Cessna late downwind.
Observation 4: At 1336:49 EST the Piper was instructed to line up on runway 17L and remained behind the displaced threshold.
Observation 5: The Sling passed overhead the lined-up Piper and completed a touch-and-go.
Observation 6: The Piper instructor reported the event after the training flight, at about 1440 EST.
