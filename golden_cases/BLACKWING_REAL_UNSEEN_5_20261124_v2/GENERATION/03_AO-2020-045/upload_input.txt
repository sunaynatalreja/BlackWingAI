Source: Australian Transport Safety Bureau; AO-2020-045. Selected factual paraphrase of public final occurrence account, not an independent preliminary report.

Observation 1: On 31 August 2020 at Carnarvon Airport the aircraft involved were Regional Express Saab 340B VH-ZRH and Shine Aviation Piper PA-31 VH-ITF.
Observation 2: Both aircraft were preparing to conduct passenger flights.
Observation 3: At approximately 1704 local time the Piper had started its take-off on runway 22.
Observation 4: The Saab entered runway 22 shortly after the Piper began rolling.
Observation 5: The Piper pilot radioed that the aircraft was rolling and rejected take-off; the Saab stopped just within the runway strip.
