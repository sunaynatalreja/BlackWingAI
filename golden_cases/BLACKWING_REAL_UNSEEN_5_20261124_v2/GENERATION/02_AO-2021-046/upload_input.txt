Source: Australian Transport Safety Bureau; AO-2021-046. Selected factual paraphrase of public final occurrence account, not an independent preliminary report.

Observation 1: On 26 October 2021 a Beech Baron 58 (VH-NSK) was preparing for a private test flight at Bankstown Airport.
Observation 2: An Embraer 190 was conducting high-power engine runs at the departure end of runway 29C.
Observation 3: The controller authorised the Baron to enter and line up on runway 29R.
Observation 4: The Baron crossed 29R and entered occupied runway 29C without clearance.
Observation 5: The controller recognised the wrong-runway entry and told the pilot to hold position; the Baron did not begin the take-off.
