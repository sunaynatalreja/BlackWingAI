Source: Australian Transport Safety Bureau; AO-2019-055. Selected factual paraphrase of public final occurrence account, not an independent preliminary report.

Observation 1: On the evening of 25 September 2019 at Canberra Airport a Virgin Australia ATR72 VH-VPJ was preparing for a scheduled passenger flight to Sydney.
Observation 2: The crew received line-up clearance for runway 35 via intersection Golf.
Observation 3: The aircraft instead lined up on runway 30 and started its take-off roll.
Observation 4: The crew rejected take-off at about the time ATC directed it to stop.
Observation 5: The aircraft was repositioned for departure from runway 35.
