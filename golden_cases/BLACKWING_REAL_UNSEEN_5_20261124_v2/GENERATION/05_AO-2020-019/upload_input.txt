Source: Australian Transport Safety Bureau; AO-2020-019. Selected factual paraphrase of public final occurrence account, not an independent preliminary report.

Observation 1: On 17 March 2020 a QantasLink Dash 8-402 VH-LQJ was preparing to depart Gladstone for Brisbane while an ATEC Faeta 321, 24-8279, was conducting circuit training.
Observation 2: The Dash 8 crew had set 126.7 MHz rather than the local 118.8 MHz CTAF.
Observation 3: At 0644:29 EST the Faeta transmitted that it was on short final to runway 10.
Observation 4: At about 0645 EST the Dash 8 taxied onto the runway for a runway 28 departure; the approaching Faeta instructor initiated a go-around.
Observation 5: The Dash 8 received a TCAS traffic advisory at 0645:42 EST and the crew later corrected the radio frequency.
