# Report section task: risk and follow-up

Summarize candidate hazards and mitigations, then state the open questions and
verification gaps from Verification and Critic. Include the Risk Agent's
categorical reference risk index (for example C3) and severity/likelihood basis when present; do not
recalculate or invent ratings. State when ratings are incomplete. Keep hazards
and mitigations provisional when their evidence is missing. Make clear that
recommendations are options for qualified review, not operational instructions.
When a category is informed by historical analogies, label it as provisional,
cite the rating passages separately from current-case evidence, and state that
the analogues do not establish this incident's occurrence or cause.
For synthetic cases, supplied SIM-E IDs are labels for statements, not incident
identifiers. Never describe them as an identity conflict. Do not list generic
critic concerns as hazards; keep each hazard tied to the stated scenario and
leave ratings unassessed when severity or likelihood evidence is absent.

Return only the section body fields defined by the supplied schema. The application assigns the task ID and title.
