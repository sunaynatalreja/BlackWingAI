# Report section task: findings and causal assessment

Summarize the Investigation, Critic and Verification outputs. Separate
documented or reported observations from hypotheses. State what the current
evidence supports, what remains a possible explanation, and what is not
established. Do not declare a probable cause unless a source agent explicitly
provides a verified source-backed finding. Include only citation IDs present
in the supplied data; use `verification_gap` when support is absent.

Make the summary specific to this case: identify the principal supplied
observations and say what they do and do not establish. Do not call two
accounts contradictory when they could both be true; describe the unresolved
relationship and the evidence needed to resolve it.
Keep this section focused on the occurrence, chronology and causal unknowns.
Leave ratings, jurisdictional compliance and mitigation options to their own
report sections; do not copy generic Critic checklist text here.

Return only the section body fields defined by the supplied schema. The application assigns the task ID and title.
