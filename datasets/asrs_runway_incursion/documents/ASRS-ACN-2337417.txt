Synopsis:
Air carrier technician reported while taxiing aircraft from the gate to the hangar; they inadvertently crossed a runway hold short line and ATC instructed a landing aircraft to go around.

Narrative:
I was taxing Aircraft X from the terminal to the hanger. I was told by ATC to cross runway XXL at taxiway 1. I think I was told to 'operate without delay' or 'expedite'. The aircraft was much heavier than usual due to extra fuel onboard. I used more than usual thrust to cross runway XXL. Once across runway XXL heading North on taxiway 1 I applied brakes to hold short of runway XXC. The aircraft brakes could not stop the jet like I was accustomed to. In addition to aircraft brakes; I was able to deploy left and right engine thrust reversers to get the plane to stop. The aircraft stopped beyond the hold bar causing a landing aircraft to do a 'go around'. Cause: I believe the event occurred due to three reasons. One; the aircraft had approximately 20K lbs more fuel than normal. Two; due to the weight of the aircraft; it's performance both on accelerating and braking was not what I have experienced in the past. Three; I was following direction to 'expedite'.Suggestions: In the future; I will tell ATC I'm unable to expedite if an aircraft is too heavy.
