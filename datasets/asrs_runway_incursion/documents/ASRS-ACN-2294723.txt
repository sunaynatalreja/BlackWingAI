Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported being told they had landed on a runway already occupied by an aircraft.

Narrative:
While I was taxiing back to Runway XX a jet that landed ahead of me on XX said I landed on an active runway.When I landed I did not see him on the runway. When I turned off the runway and was clear of the Runway XX I announced it on the radio; I looked both ways for taxiway traffic; and proceeded to then turn onto the taxiway back to Runway XX. I then received the call on the radio asking me for my tail #. The person on the radio asked me if I landed on an active runway. I responded that I did not see any plane on the runway when I landed. I do not believe I landed on an active runway.
