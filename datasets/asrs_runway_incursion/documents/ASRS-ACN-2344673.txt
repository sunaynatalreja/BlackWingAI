Synopsis:
General aviation Flight Instructor reported a near miss during final approach with two ground vehicles sitting on the runway. Flight Instructor performed a go-around.

Narrative:
Me and my student turned final for runway 17 at ELA; we began to notice some red lights that were out of place on the runway. We made calls on frequency asking if anyone was on the runway and decided to do a low pass after no answer. As we were passing at about 300' AGL we began to see 2 vehicles move and exit the runway; as they exited we could see that these vehicles were police vehicles. There were 2 police vehicles sitting on the runway with no red/blue or hazard lights. The only reason we saw them was because one car had its brake lights on. After the pass and watching them both clear the runway we decided to land. Under slightly different circumstances something like this could end bad quick; say we were landing on the side of the runway closer to them and then our landing light not work this could have ended very bad very quick. Summary is that 2 law enforcement vehicles were sitting on a dark runway at night with no hazard lights on.
