Synopsis:
Cessna Turbo Stationair 6 Flight Instructor reported during landing roll out at a non towered airport; they took evasive action to avoid an aircraft landing opposite direction.

Narrative:
During a company training flight we conducted pattern work at ZZZ on RWY XX. After departing to the east for a simulated engine fire at 7;500'; we returned for a landing on RWY XY. I monitored and announced on CTAF; no other traffic was heard or observed via ADS-B in the vicinity during the descent or approach.Upon touchdown on RWY XY; I observed an unidentified aircraft on short final (approximately 5-10' AGL) for RWY XX; landing in the opposite direction. I immediately took flight controls; decelerated; and taxied clear of the runway to provide the other aircraft space to land or go around. Given the length of the runway; the closest proximity between the two aircraft was approximately 4;000'.I attempted to contact the aircraft on CTAF three times with no response. The aircraft taxied back and departed RWY XX without making any radio transmissions. Our radio was confirmed functional; as we had been in contact with other traffic 30 minutes prior. The registration number was not obtainable during the fly-over.
