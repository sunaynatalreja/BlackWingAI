Synopsis:
PA-28 pilot reported being issued a go around by ATC due to lining up on the wrong runway.

Narrative:
During the initial approach into ZZZ ATC advised that I will be expecting Runway XXL where I will need to do a right hand turn to set up for XXL at ZZZ. Taking that into consideration I planned to make XXL as my landing point. While turning from downwind leg to the base leg ATC advised me to watch out for the Airbus. While acknowledging the aircaft; I heard on the transmission regarding a change to Runway XXR and right closed traffic approved for XXR. I assumed this was for me since I was on base and immediately turned to final and my goal was to do a touch and go but I failed to verify with ATC which aircraft it was for. On the Final Approach just as I was near the threshold I saw the citation enter the runway and immediately issued the go around. At that point ATC told me to go around and reported possible pilot deviation.
