Synopsis:
Small aircraft pilot reported while taking off; an airport mower entered the runway resulting with the aircraft overflying the mower.

Narrative:
On the takeoff roll; I noticed a mower appear from behind hangars on the east side of the runway moving toward the runway. The mower was moving rapidly. The mower entered the runway as I was lifting off the runway; about 100ft ahead of me. I flew over the mower by about 15 feet. As I flew over him; the mower appeared to see me; and swerved back into the grassy runway safety area.There was no chance that I would have been able to stop the plane in time for the mower had I aborted the takeoff; and that's why I decided to continue with the takeoff. It happened very quickly; and the only safe option was to takeoff. I recommend the FAA review the current runway safety practices for ground vehicles at 8W2 airport.
