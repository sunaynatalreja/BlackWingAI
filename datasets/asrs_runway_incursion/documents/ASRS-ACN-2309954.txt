Synopsis:
General aviation Designated Pilot Examiner reported a traffic conflict with another aircraft resulting in a rejected takeoff during a check ride. The check pilot took control and stopped the aircraft.

Narrative:
During a check ride with a private pilot applicant; the Tower switched us from Runway XX to Runway XY. I instructed the applicant to demonstrate a short field take off. The Tower cleared us to line up and wait. Unfortunately I did not hear the transmission while the applicant started a takeoff. During the beginning role I spotted another cessna taxiing across the runway for which I stopped the aircraft. We visited the Tower and admitted our mistake. In hindsight I realized the student had a habit of turning down radio volume in order to hear better the intercom. I also realized; as an examiner with years of experience; the importance of ignoring distractions during this critical phase of flight.
