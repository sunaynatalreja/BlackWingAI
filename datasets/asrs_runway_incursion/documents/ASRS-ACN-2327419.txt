Synopsis:
Light jet Captain reported a runway incursion at BOS related to misunderstanding the taxi clearance. Aircraft on final was sent around.

Narrative:
Arriving BOS. Landed RWY 33L. Exited with left turn onto taxiway T. While still rolling to clear runway; ATC instructed taxi M;F; hold short 4L. Crew thought instructions were right turn M; F; hold short 4L. Realizing this was incorrect and taxiway F was behind aircraft; crew stopped the aircraft on M short of RWY 33L and immediately notified Ground. Aircraft on final to RWY 33L did a go-around. Snow covered taxiways and snow banks obscured taxiway signs and taxiway markings. Crew did not see hold short lines. Suggestions: Stop aircraft after exiting runway if ANY doubt exists on instructions. Recognize task saturation with busy airport and hurried instructions. ATC may not realize you aren't a regular visitor to their fine establishment. Brief not only possible taxiway exits from landing runway; but consequential taxi routes that result from choices. Make note of nonstandard hold short lines in brief.
