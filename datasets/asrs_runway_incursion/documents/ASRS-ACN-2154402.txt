Synopsis:
Light aircraft student pilot reported taking off and flying over another light aircraft that was back-taxiing on his takeoff runway.

Narrative:
I was lined up and holding short of runway 36 at 1C5 ready for takeoff. The plane that had just landed on runway 36 made a radio call that was difficult to hear and I thought they said they were clear of the runway. I glanced down the runway; did not see the plane and made my takeoff call. During my takeoff run; I did not see the plane. Just after liftoff; I saw the plane off the front right side of my windshield and realized they were still on the runway and were back taxiing and about to turn off of the runway. These are some steps I plan on taking next time to avoid this situation:1. If the radio call is choppy or unclear; ask the pilot to repeat their call. 2. Make sure to have my eyes set more ahead and down the runway during takeoff. 3. Make sure to glance at the runway for a longer period of time and more thoroughly check if I can go ahead and taxi onto the runway for takeoff.

Secondary Narrative:
My student was doing solo time in the pattern. I was at the airport monitoring him but was outside of the airplane so my student was PIC. My student was lined up for takeoff and the plane that had just landed made a radio call that was unclear. My student thought that the call was to communicate that they were clear of the runway.  My student took a quick look down the runway; did not see anybody and proceeded to make his takeoff call. He then taxied onto the runway and took off. He realized that there was a plane still back taxiing on the runway once he was in the air and still had sight of the runway.Steps I would take to prevent this in the future:1. Have more lessons where we fly to busier airports so that they are more used to dealing busy situations in the traffic pattern and on the ground. 2. Making sure my student always asks for a pilot or controller to repeat any calls that are unclear to them.
