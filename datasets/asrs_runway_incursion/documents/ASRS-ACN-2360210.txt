Synopsis:
Citation Latitude Captain reported a traffic conflict with an aircraft on downwind when crossing the hold short line at a non-towered airport.

Narrative:
We were holding short of runway XX at ZZZ on the east side of the runway. We had just received an IFR release from air traffic control who gave us a three minute void time. There was one aircraft on downwind for runway XX. We made a radio call to the common traffic advisory frequency to advise that we were going to take the runway for departure. As we began to power up past the hold short line; the first officer said stop stop. I stopped the aircraft with the nose just passed the hold short line for runway XX at the approach end. As I looked over the shoulder of my first officer towards the 3 o'clock of our airplane; I saw a C130 approaching low and fast on a severely short approach. The C130 operator had not advised us of his intention to make a short approach. Due to the close proximity and high approach speed of the C130 I chose to stop and remain where we were and advise the C130 our nose was just passed the hold short line. The C130 made a radio call on the common traffic advisory frequency to say they were on left base. In the interest of safety and out of an abundance of caution; I chose to allow the C130 to execute his procedure first before taking the runway. The C130 accomplished a touch and go in front of us and then afterwards we departed without incident.Suggestions: It's incredibly important; especially in an uncontrolled airport environment to be aware of the type of aircraft sharing the airspace. This particular C130 was a firefighting tanker out doing practice maneuvers. The aircraft commander of the C130 never indicated to us the type of aircraft they were or the nature of their fighter pilot type of approach that they were planning on accomplishing. The aircraft descended very quickly from 1600 feet above the runway on a downwind in a hard left bank straight to the runway threshold. This type of approach caught us off guard as we assumed a normal approaching landing which would have given us more than enough time to depart safely without conflict. I'm thankful to the first officer for having his head on a swivel and calling out the closure rate of the surprise maneuver.
