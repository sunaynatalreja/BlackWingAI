Synopsis:
Air carrier Captain reported during takeoff roll a general aviation aircraft taxied across the MYNN/NAS Runway 28. Captain stated the Airport Moving Map and Jeppesen airport diagrams were different; with the former showing hold-short lines.

Narrative:
Question regarding NAS Taxiway Hotel and Runway 28. We were cleared for takeoff. On takeoff roll around 110 kt.; both of us observed a GA aircraft taxiing across the runway on what we assumed was Taxiway H. We continued the takeoff. At cruise we studied both the AMM (Airport Moving Map) and Jepp airport diagrams. AMM showed hold-short lines. Jepp did not. It is probable that NAS Ground considers it a non-issue. But it is confusing at first and could be mistaken for a runway incursion. Seems some clarification in the 10-7 page would help.
