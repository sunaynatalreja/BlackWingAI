Synopsis:
Flight Instructor reported they followed another aircraft for landing; lost sight of the aircraft; and proceeded to land while the other aircraft was still on the runway.

Narrative:
I was training a commercial student on his last Power Off 180 before his check ride. We were following another training aircraft doing a touch and go on runway XX. The G1000 screen showed a separation of about .75 miles; which we kept on purpose. As the leading aircraft continued it's roll on the runway on take off; we were on ground effect with the nose high and I lost sight of the leading aircraft; which I believed had taken off. When my student touched down I realized that the leading aircraft had not yet got airborne; was still rolling for take off.
