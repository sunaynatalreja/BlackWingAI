Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported a near miss with another aircraft when landing on a parallel runway at a non-towered airport. The did not see the other aircraft enter the runway and the other pilot was not communicating their position.

Narrative:
On day 0 I was towing gliders for a company.  During one of the landings (we land on glider 8 while traffic uses main runway 8; I made my base call; final call and short final call.  Upon landing rollout I noticed a plane departing the runway on the upwind leg.  I saw the plane holding short of the runway but did not hear any callouts or see them enter the runway.  I am not sure if they did not hear my call outs.  I was down and stopped by the time they passed me so there was no danger of collision.  I will work on situational awareness and my scan of the environment in case it was my fault.  If it was the other planes fault not much I could have done.
