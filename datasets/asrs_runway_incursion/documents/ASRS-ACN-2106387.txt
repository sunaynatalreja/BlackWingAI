Synopsis:
Air carrier Captain reported seeing an airport vehicle on the runway during takeoff roll. Flight crew continued takeoff and overflew vehicle.

Narrative:
While operating a Part 91 reposition flight from ZZZ-ZZZ1; we were given take off clearance from the tower to take off Runway XX. After we began the takeoff roll and past V1; I observed an orange airport vehicle (pickup truck) on the runway in the vicinity of Taxiway 1. It was impossible to stop at that point and we continued the takeoff and overflew the vehicle as it attempted to exit the runway at Taxiway 1. We reported the event to the Control Tower after we departed. We continued on to ZZZ1 and landed uneventfully.It is likely the truck was working on the approach lighting system on the departure end of the runway and was on the runway at the time we commenced our takeoff roll. It was not possible for the flight crew to see the far end of the runway due to elevation differences of the runway surface.
