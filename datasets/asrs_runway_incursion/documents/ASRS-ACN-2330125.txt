Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported a ground conflict on takeoff which resulted in the Runway Status Light System illuminating red centerline lights while the aircraft was on the takeoff roll; causing the Captain to reject the takeoff. After stopping on the runway; an aircraft landed on the intersecting runway.

Narrative:
Runway 27 clearance to line up and wait red stop bar lights illuminated.  Takeoff clearance was given red stop bar lights extinguished; on takeoff roll runway red stop lights illuminated.  I could not see traffic landing on runway 33L.  I rejected takeoff stopping prior to runway 33L intersection.  Aircraft landed on runway 33L we taxied clear of runway 27 returning to gate for maintenance inspection.  After maintenance inspection we departed to ZZZ.  Cause: Could have more separation from ATC with aircraft on visual approaches to intersecting runways.

Secondary Narrative:
On takeoff from runway 27 at BOS we were issues a line up and wait clearance. All procedures leading up to the event were normal. The captain aligned the aircraft on runway and then transferred controls to me while we waited for takeoff clearance. The red RSLS (Runway Status Light System) lights were illuminated while we waited and then extinguished. We the. Received our takeoff clearance. Shortly after 80 knots the red status lights illuminated once again; the captain promptly took control of the aircraft and rejected around roughly 100 knots. The aircraft came to a stop before the intersecting runway 33L. Tower was notified of the reject and they instructed us to hold short of 33L for the landing traffic. After the landing traffic we taxied off of the runway to discuss with M/X (Maintenance) the next course of action before returning to the gate. Tower was also informed the reason for the go around being the illumination of the red status lights indicating to us that the runway was no longer safe and an incursion from somewhere was possible. Cause: I do not know exactly what caused the lights to illuminate; if it was the landing traffic on the crossing runway being too close; potential other traffic taxiing; or a failure of the system. Either way all aircraft were kept safe and the system instructed us to stop and in doing so any potential conflict; if one did exist; was avoided.
