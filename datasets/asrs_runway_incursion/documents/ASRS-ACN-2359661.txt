Synopsis:
General aviation Flight Instructor reported a critical ground conflict with an aircraft on approach.

Narrative:
PA30 was approaching the airport and announced a 5 mile final. Cessna proceeded to Runway XX Hold short line and completed final checks for departure. The Cessna crew scanned the sky for traffic but did not see the PA30 on final.  The PA30 announced a 2-mile Final; the Cessna pilots visually scanned the final approach; did not see traffic and immediately proceeded towards the runway for departure.  The Cessna pilot made a radio call 'taking Runway XX for departure'.  The PA30 pilot shouted 'Hey we are on short final; what are you doing!'  The Cessna pilot immediately stopped the aircraft approximately 150 feet from the runway edge but beyond the hold short line. The left seat pilot looked again; saw the PA30 and judged the aircraft to be about 1 inch wide on the windscreen. The pilots exchange dialogue for a few moments to verify positions; and eventually the PA30 executed a go-around  which seemed to be several hundred feet above the threshold; Contributing factors: Overcast; grey sky; and Cessna was position perpendicular to the departing runway.  Lessons Learned: Angle the aircraft prior to the hold short line as appropriate to get a clear view of the final approach course. It may have been possible to see the PA30's landing lights.Human Factors: Cessna crew was alert; fatigue was not a factor. Cessna crew used pre-takeoff checklist; and briefed the takeoff prior to departure. The crew heard the distance calls and judged and concurred the separation to be safe and sufficient for departure.
