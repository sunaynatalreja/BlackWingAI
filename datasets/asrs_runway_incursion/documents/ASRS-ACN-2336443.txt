Synopsis:
Pilot reported landing on a closed runway at LFK but did not know until the following day. The closed runway was not mentioned in the ASOS broadcast; NOTAMs; and there were no visible runway closure lighting signs.

Narrative:
Before landing at LFK I listened to ASOS and checked weather and NOTAMs. No NOTAM for Runway 16 that I landed safely in. But the following day I learned that Runway 16 was closed. Also no runway (X) closure visible lighting signs on runway or on the ASOS broadcast. I feel that all of this could have been avoided if more safety precautions from the FBO had been taken. Moving forward I would recommend for the FBO to enhance safety measures by providing pilots with more information and (X) lighting signs on each end of the runway.
