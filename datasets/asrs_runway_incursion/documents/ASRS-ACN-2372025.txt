Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported there are confusing markings in green on the taxiway at ABQ.

Narrative:
After landing; taxiing to E. Cleared by ground to hold short of Runway 3. Approaching a strange intersection of multiple taxiways; I saw green markings on the ground; and other what appeared to be non-standard markings on the taxiway; even the color green. It was not intuitive and I could not see a runway; rather it looked like just taxiways intersecting. The actual runway threshold is over 300 yards away from that point. I slowed to stop as I was confused with what I saw and intended to stop and ask ground for progressive taxi. I said on Intercommunications System (ICS) to the CFI in the back seat; Am I supposed to stop here;" as I was coming to a stop; and he responded; "No continue to that dog leg to the left." At that point I believe my nose was on or near the hold short line and I heard from ground; Aircraft X no delay cross Runway 3; at which point I continued to taxi on E. I saw what looked like what used to be a hold short line closer to Runway 3 and eventually saw the runway environment and overrun at the departure end of Runway 3. Contributing factors: confusing and non-intuitive hold short and taxiway markings that were not near the runway or like anything I had ever seen; I was stopping at the hold short line when my CFI told me to continue as it was apparently confusing to him as well."
