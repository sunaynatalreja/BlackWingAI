Synopsis:
Aircraft Captain reported a ground vehicle on the runway during approach. Pilot executed a missed approach.

Narrative:
I was on the approach into OOK airport; already cleared for the approach from on the ground in ZZZZ. I was in contact with another aircraft in the vicinity that was staying out of my way for the approach. As I was inbound; I started making a 10-mile; 5-mile; and 3-mile final call. At minimums; I continued the approach as I had runway environment in sight; as I continued the descent and prepared for landing; I made out a large grader in the middle of the runway on the first third. I immediately did a go around and went missed. After getting myself sorted; I called up the center and explained that I was on the published missed approach. They asked why I went missed; and I said there was a grader on the runway; and asked if I would like to shoot the approach again. The grader then came on the radio and let me know that he was now clear of the runway; and that he had his radio volume turned down; so he didn't hear our calls. On my second approach; I landed without issue and took off without incident.Cause: The cause of this incident was the grader's radios being turned down to where he did not hear my inbound radio calls. This; paired with low visibility and ceilings; left me in the dark about whether he was in the runway environment.Suggestions: Suggestions that could prevent this event from occurring again would be to make sure that the grader's are required to have their radios on and turned up when on the runway; or moving in the runway environment.
