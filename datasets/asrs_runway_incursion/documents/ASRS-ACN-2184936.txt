Synopsis:
Fractional jet Captain reported turning onto a crossing runway during landing causing a go-around for traffic landing on that runway.

Narrative:
We were given instructions on very short final and advised read back not necessary. After touch down we were given more instructions during control transfer. We interpreted these instructions to mean clear runway immediately for traffic landing behind us. We exited runway 16 onto first available which was runway 11. This caused a go around for traffic landing simultaneously on 11. We were not able to gain situational awareness as to what the controller was doing in the short time period or what was expected of us from here instructions. At no point in instructions was traffic landing on crossing runway conveyed.
