Synopsis:
GA flight instructor with student reported a runway incursion at 1N7; a non-towered airport; resulting in an aircraft on final approach having to execute a go-around.

Narrative:
I was in Aircraft X crossing the runway at Blairstown. At the time we had chosen to cross the runway; the area was safe. The glider operations were still setting up and Aircraft Y was just turning base. We cross the hold short line after indicating our intentions on the CTAF. After that; the glider tow; Aircraft Z; indicates on the CTAF that they were taking off on the grass strip. We are forced to apply hard braking to stop before crossing Aircraft Z's path. The Aircraft Z stops after being advised by the glider that we intended to cross. We let Aircraft Z go out of caution due to his lack of situational awareness. Meanwhile Aircraft Y is now on final and continuing its approach while we are stationary on the runway waiting for Aircraft Z to take off. This leaves us little time to then quickly evacuate the paved runway before Aircraft Y lands. I believe Aircraft Z had put us into a bad situation where we were well beyond the hold short line before they had then decided to take off. This then left us stuck in Aircraft Y's path while sitting on runway 25 with no room to turn off. I believe Aircraft Y should have also chosen to go around while seeing us stranded inside the paved runway protected area. Instead they had chosen to force the landing. Actions we could have taken was to wait for Aircraft Z to go and for Aircraft Y to land; but as mentioned previously; we had clearly indicated our intention to use the runway surface well before Aircraft Y was on final approach; and before the Aircraft Z had indicated their intention to take off.
