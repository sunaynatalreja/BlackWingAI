Synopsis:
Pitts pilot reported indications of a main wheel brake locking up on landing; causing a ground loop.

Narrative:
I am the pilot/owner of Pitts S1S; Aircraft X. Upon returning to ZZZ from a short VFR pleasure flight; I was cleared for a visual approach to Runway XXL at ZZZ. The approach and landing were normal. However; immediately after touchdown; the right main tire chattered and squealed as if the brake was locked up. The aircraft began pulling hard to the right and I countered with left rudder input in an attempt to maintain directional control of the aircraft. To no avail; the aircraft continued right and as it slowed it began a clockwise ground loop. The right main tire lifted off the ground and the left wingtip and outboard side of the left aileron scrapped the ground as the aircraft came to a stop with the tail wheel about three feet off the runway and the nose pointing southbound.Tower radioed me regarding my status and I told them I was OK and will attempt to taxi the aircraft clear of the runway. I taxied the aircraft a very short distance onto taxiway; shutdown and secured the aircraft systems. I egressed the aircraft and inspected the airplane. The right main tire was flat and the tail wheel assembly was damaged and bent from the side load during the ground loop. I was not injured during this event. Subsequently; ZZZ aircraft operations met me and we proceeded to transport the aircraft back to my hanger after talking to FAA and NTSB personnel which the Tower had contacted.
