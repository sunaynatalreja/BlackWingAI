Synopsis:
C-172 pilot reported runway incursion with another aircraft preparing to land and forced to do a go-around at a non-towered airport.

Narrative:
Runway incursion with many aircraft in pattern and after waiting for landing and clearing 3 aircraft called for departure on Runway XX and heard nothing after turning plane and checking final pulled out on runway and started departure. Just before starting climb seen plane come over to the right of runway. Talked to the plane and said I did not hear his calls with him answering a lot of radio traffic not only at ZZZ but at altitude the other airports on same frequency. At ZZZ I normally do a 360 degree swing to verify downwind and any close base aircraft in pattern but with 3 planes behind me waiting did not do the swing.
