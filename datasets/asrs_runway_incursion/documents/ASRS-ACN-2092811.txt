Synopsis:
Air carrier pilot reported a runway incursion due to crossing the runway hold short line which was displaced on the taxiway well short of the runway. The pilot taxiing the aircraft was confused by sun glare and taxied past the hold short line up to the runway.

Narrative:
I was reading off the before takeoff checklist below the line and when I looked up; we were crossed the hold short line. I said something and the Captain said he got confused by the sun glare. He thought the hold short line was still up ahead but he couldn't tell because he was looking right into the sun. The airport had moved the hold short line to a place that was much further away and closer to the ramp from where a normal hold short line is located. There wasn't an aircraft on the runway or landing so there was no conflict.
