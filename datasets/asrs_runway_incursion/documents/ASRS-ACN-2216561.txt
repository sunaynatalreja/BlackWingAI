Synopsis:
CE-560XLS flight crew crossed a runway without clearance and had a near miss with another aircraft.

Narrative:
We were not in anyway hurried or operating at an unreasonable pace.  We landed on XXL ZZZ. We were unfamiliar on where the FBO was.  There was a discussion to the tower operator and he says it's a bit confusing and he would give a progressive.  As we cleared the runway XXL.  and under the knowing we would be guided to the FBO; I let down my guard. I didn't hear the taxi clearance clearly; missing the hold short of XXR…a parallel runway quite close to the runway I was exiting from on taxiway 1.  I cleared and tracked straight; looked left and I missed the hold short to make sure that area was clear and then before I could get the words 'clear left' clear to cross (same time bringing my head around to look at the opposite direction; I was stunned to see a small training aircraft (a diamond) climbing (estimated to be 200; feet or higher) overhead on the runway I thought we were cleared to cross without issue.  My FO was 'heads down completing the after landing checklist and was surprised to see the same; not knowing I hadn't heard the clearances in its entirety and I was taxiing beyond the hold bars.  I had chose to cross as stopping could potentially be more harmful if other planes were to follow.  We realizes then we crossed an active runway without clearance.  GND/TOWER was professional; and allowed us to progress to a safe location; communicated what we understood to have happened and gave us a telephone number; and then continued to give us the directions to the FBO.I called the telephone number given. Accepted responsibility without excuse and he gave me what his responsibilities were.  Later; when we were ready to continue with our next trip; a further exchange of info occurred.  (My name and contact info); and the rest of the trip and day went without further incidents.Suggestions: I admit that I fallen into a 'guard down state;' that finding the FBO wasn't going to be a search; and that in that I missed the part of the instructions (and read back) to hold short of XXL.  Had I taken the time to communicate that to my FO; by stopping and asking.  No matter how short the space between the runways; this would have avoided the incident. We also discussed and agree that no checklist is necessarily important enough it can't wait til cleared of all runways.

Secondary Narrative:
After we landed on RWY XXL at ZZZ we were instructed to clear the RWY on taxiway 1 and Hold short of RWY XXR; we repeated the instructions back.After clearing the runway I performed the after landing flow and checklist. When I looked up from those duties; I saw we were already past the holdshort line for RWY XXR.Suggestions: Wait until clearing all parallel runways before performing any activities that can distract the Pilot monitoring.
