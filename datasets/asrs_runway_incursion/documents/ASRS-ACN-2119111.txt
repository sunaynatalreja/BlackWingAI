Synopsis:
Flight Instructor and student reported during solo flight student taxiied onto runway without clearance; resulting in ATC directing a go-around for an aircraft on final approach.

Narrative:
During first student pilot solo flight and after completing run up proceeded on taxi way 1 to continue taxi to Runway XXR. I was holding short of Runway XXR. ATC advised me that 'hold short of Runway XXR.'; and I replied 'hold short of Runway XXR.' But; due to already holding short of Runway XXR; I didn't expect to be advised to hold short. It set me [to] have an expected bias that I was told to 'line up and wait.' even though I replied 'hold short.' After that proceeded on Runway XXR and lined up and waited. Tower called an aircraft on final to go around and told me that I was supposed to hold short. After that I exited taxiway 2 and was instructed to copy phone number and returned back to ramp.

Secondary Narrative:
My student was on his first solo flight. After conducting run up at ZZZ; ground instructed him to continue taxi and monitor tower. As he was in sequence for Runway XXR and number one now. Tower instructed him to 'hold short Runway XXR'; he correctly replied 'Hold short Runway XXR' and then proceeded on to the runway to line up and wait. Tower recognized what happened and sent the aircraft on final around and instructed my student to exit on taxiway 2 and contact ground. The student suffered from expectation biased that when he is number one holding short he will probably be given takeoff clearance. He was given a phone number by Tower for possible pilot deviation. The student was briefed on the matter and remedial training has been initiated.
