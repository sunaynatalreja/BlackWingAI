Synopsis:
Corporate first officer reported conflict traffic taxied onto active runway resulted in rejected take off and a critical ground conflict.

Narrative:
Departing runway 28R full length at SFO we had a rejected takeoff due to Aircraft Y taxiing onto the runway in front of us. Aircraft Y had turned right on the 'E' taxiway onto the runway instead of left onto the taxiway per their taxi clearance. After we began our takeoff roll we noticed the runway safety lights illuminate red. In the few seconds between this and the time we processed the meaning of the lights ATC canceled our clearance and we aborted on the runway safely. Estimated speed at time of about was about 70; still within the low speed regime and we were able to quickly clear the runway onto taxiway N. The incursion by Aircraft Y also caused closely trailing traffic that was landing to have to go around. Crew discussed brake cooling and applicable procedures and due to low energy nature and very low takeoff weight determined that extended cooling times were not required. After reconfig; a short wait and brief the next takeoff was uneventful.What caused this -I can only speculate but it appeared they had been flying a long day with numerous legs so fatigue likely was a factor. How we could improve- There was a short period during the roll where the status lights had illuminated but we had not fully processed their meaning and applicable procedure. Likely due in part to lack of exposure to this system as Part 91 pilots mostly utilizing airports not so equipped.Recommend company safety bulletin on runway status lights and possibly integrating this scenario into sim training instead of the usual red hold short light scenario that training utilizes.
