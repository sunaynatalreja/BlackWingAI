Synopsis:
Air traffic controller reported aircraft not following taxi instructions resulting in a runway incursion and canceling takeoff clearance to another aircraft to avoid a collision.

Narrative:
I was working Local control west position and owned runway XXL and runway XY. We were landing runway XXL and departing runway XY. It was IFR and snowing; visibility was 3/4SM.  I had Aircraft X cross runway XY at 1 taxing to runway XXL. Aircraft Y was lined up on runway XY. My next transmission was for Aircraft X to cross runway XXL and 2. Once Aircraft X finished the crossing I issued a clear for takeoff clearance to Aircraft Y. Since no one was on final for runway XXL I was able to line up Aircraft Z on runway XXL. I scanned the asde-x and noticed Aircraft X didn't make the turn on 2 and was heading to runway XY. I tried to reach out and have Aircraft X stop and then my next transmission was Aircraft Y cancel takeoff clearance. When Aircraft Y read back the cancel takeoff clearance the asde-x alarmed through out the tower warning and highlighted the 2 aircraft on the asde-x. Once I was able to see Aircraft Y came to a stop I asked them if they were able to go back to the runway or needed other assistance. They said they the were able to taxi back to the runway but would need a few minutes. I knew I had to continue working the position. Since Aircraft X  was in the intersection of runway XY at 1 so the best thing to do was keep him going on 1 and send him to runway XXL. Next I had to clear up runway XXL and issue takeoff clearance to Aircraft Z. Once Aircraft Z was airborne I taxied Aircraft Y back to runway XY with a right turn on runway XXL and a right turn on taxiway 2. Both Aircraft Y and Aircraft X departed at XA:57Z and XB:58Z. I asked for a break moments after it happened and due to short staffing it wasn't available at that time. About 10 minutes later a controller came up the stairs from break and was able to get me out of position. I was asked to take a short break because the controller on clearance delivery was almost at 2 hours on position. I took a 20 minute break and came back to work clearance. Within about 10 minutes on clearance I asked the supervisor if I could go home when they would get me out. At XC:27Z I was able to get off position and then went home. .
