Synopsis:
Airport personnel reported a runway sweeper vehicle turned onto a runway with an aircraft on final approach. Aircraft performed a go-around.

Narrative:
I was told to escort a sweeper to sweep taxiways 1 and 2 as well as to escort the sweeper across the runway to clear debris. I spoke with the sweeper operator and told him that he had to sweep 1 first and then we would move onto 2 after I received clearance from the tower and then the runway afterwards. Typically; I would be in front of the vehicle I am escorting but for this instance I could not as the sweeper was flinging debris. I radioed the ground frequency and had confirmation to clean 1. The sweeper operator began to clean 1 but then mistakenly turned down Taxiway 2. As soon as the operator turned down taxiway 2; I immediately went after him to try and stop him; but I could not. He had crossed the runway with an aircraft on final before I could stop him. The aircraft pilot initiated a go around without incident.After this incident we had a safety standdown meeting that I attended with both my company as well as the construction company including the sweeper operator. During this meeting we went over the root cause of the miscommunication between me and the sweeper operator that had occurred. We also developed means and methods to make sure this never happens again. Following the training we immediately implemented the use of two-way radios between the escort and the operators being escorted. Additionally; all operations folks attended a training to review airport paving markings and the importance and danger of crossing an active taxiway or runway.
