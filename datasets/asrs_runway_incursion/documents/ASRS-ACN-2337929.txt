Synopsis:
PA-28R pilot reported cancelling takeoff per instruction from ATC as there was traffic on the runway.

Narrative:
This morning I was having issues with my electrical system in the Arrow I was flying. My radio comms failed and I had talked with the tower over the phone about me receiving my takeoff clearance over the cell phone and using that as two way comms until I was out of the airspace. I was proceeding to an uncontrolled field as my final destination. During my first takeoff attempt I was nervous and accidentally turned left onto Runway XX instead of right onto XY. As I began to throttle up to take off; tower told me cancel takeoff clearance so I immediately idled the power and applied brakes. That was my evasive action when told to cancel my takeoff. Tower told me to make a 180 so I did and taxied off the runway. There was traffic using Runway XZ.After taxiing back to the ramp and recomposing myself; I attempted again but using the correct runway and proceeded to my final destination safely. All while using the telephone to get my takeoff clearance.The series of events that led up to this event was as followed: I had to spend the night due to an electrical issue the night prior where seemingly my battery died. Mind you I had no issues with the electrical system prior to the evening startup attempt. After having it looked at by an airframe and power plant mechanic the next day (today); we addressed the issue and all seemed as normal. I went to take off and when I went to bring my wheels up; lost all electrics in the plane. So I landed and had the system examined again. It was returned to service and I had talked to tower on the telephone about taking off; maintaining VFR; and proceeding to my destination without talking to anyone. I held short of the runway and was handed off to tower and that's when the bit where I am filing this report happened. I was noting my departure instructions; trying to maintain contact via telephone; and get myself going back home so the plane could be examined by the mechanic at my home field. Task saturation and nerves occurred here. I should have remained more calm and just taken one step at a time and thought through the situation more thoroughly instead of trying to be in a hurry to get home as soon as possible trying to avoid weather that would envelope my home field for the next week judging by the forecast.After another attempt; I was able to safely take off and proceed enroute as planned.
