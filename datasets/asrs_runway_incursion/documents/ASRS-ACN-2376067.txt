Synopsis:
Citation jet C525 Captain reported a conflict with a departing aircraft when the Captain's aircraft was on final approach at a non-towered airport. The Captain performed a 360 degree turn at low altitude to provide separation from the departing aircraft.

Narrative:
Flight from ZZZ1 to ZZZ in a Citation M2. On the RNAV XX approach talking to ZZZ approach while also making radio calls to ZZZ CTAF we heard a few aircraft holding short waiting for their IFR release after we cancelled/landed. After breaking out of the clouds at approximately 1200ft AGL we cancelled IFR with ZZZ approach. We then made the permanent switch to ZZZ CTAF and continued to make position/intention reports. While on approximately a 1.5nm final to RWY XX a Citation XLS holding short of the runway XX began rolling across the hold short line taking the runway for departure. We began to sidestep to the right for a go-around. The XLS began its take off roll so we elected to make a right hand 360* turn back onto final to avoid further conflict with the XLS. We made the right 360* turn back into the 1 mile final uneventfully and landed. The other couple of aircraft holding short noted that they were unsure why the XLS decided to take off; but we are sure the crew may have lost situational awareness of the airport traffic.
