Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported a conflict while taxiing at SFO after landing requiring an aircraft on approach to execute an ATC directed go-around.

Narrative:
After landing and clearing 28L at Kilo; ground instruction was to taxi Bravo and hold short of Quebec. Then it was B; Z; hold short 10R at Z1. After several minutes; Ground cleared us to cross 10R at Z1; S3; S to the fork. As we started taxiing past the hold short line for 10R; Ground abruptly instructed us to hold short 10R. We told Ground that we were already on 10R and it was too late. This directed Tower to send the aircraft on approach to 28L for a go-around. We then were cleared to continue crossing 10R and S3; S; to the fork to hold for more aircraft under tow.

Secondary Narrative:
We landed on 28L and were instructed to exit Taxiway Killo. We switched to Ground Control and were instructed to Taxi via B to Z to Z1 and hold short 10R. We were told to be ready to cross 10R. After a short wait; Ground Control gave taxi instructions to cross 10R via Z1 to S3 to S and hold short of the 'Y'. We began our taxi and started onto the runway when Ground told us to hold short of 10R. We explained that it was too late; we were already on 10R.ATC told an aircraft on approach for 10R to go around; followed by instructions for us to continue to taxi across 10R S3  to S and hold short of Y.An additional factor was that with the initial instructions to hold short 10R at Z1; the room available at Z1 is limited; preventing a widebody from being 90 degrees to the runway. When we were holding short of 10R we were actually facing at a 45 away from arrival traffic to 28L. We could not see the aircraft on final approach. We complied with all taxi instructions promptly. This wasn't a runway incursion; but rather poor coordination between ATC; Ground; and Tower Control. They simply didn't have enough time to attempt to cross a widebody in that short of a window.
