Synopsis:
AW109 helicopter pilot reported a ground conflict after landing with an aircraft on approach.

Narrative:
Several aircraft were converging while conducting approaches for runway XX.Aircraft X was number 3 in the sequence on approach via 'ZZZZZ' for left base runway XX and carried out a delaying manuever relative to the preceding traffic.Aircraft X was on approach for right base runway XX via 'ZZZZZ1'; number 4 in sequence behind Aircraft X.The AFIS (Airborne Flight Information System) operator having Aircraft X in sight asked Aircraft Y to confirm visual contact with Aircraft X; which was confirmed.Aircraft X landed slightly before taxiway 1 and then taxied along the runway to vacate via taxiway 1While Aircraft Y was on short final for runway XX; the AFIS operator informed the Aircraft Y Captain three times of the helicopter's presence on the runway.Aircraft Y then decided to continue the landing and passed very close to Aircraft X while it was vacating the runway via taxiway 1.No other consequences were observed.
