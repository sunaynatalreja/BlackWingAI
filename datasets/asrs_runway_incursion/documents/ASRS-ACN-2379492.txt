Synopsis:
Tower controllers reported distraction and failure to get a readback of takeoff clearance with traffic landing on an occupied runway resulted in a ground conflict.

Narrative:
Aircraft X called inbound and was instructed with a right downwind entry for runway XXR.  At XA:29:59Z; Aircraft Y was instructed to LUAW (Line Up and Wait) on runway XXR for traffic departing ahead of them and also informed of the next arrival 3 miles west (Aircraft X). At XA:30:13Z; I cleared Aircraft Y for takeoff; but only after listening to the voice recorder I learned Aircraft Y never acknowledged the clearance. Having expected Aircraft Y heard the takeoff clearance and acknowledged; I moved on to other tasks and coordination with ground control. At XA:30:31Z; I issued Aircraft X their landing clearance to runway XXR with their request for a short approach. Around this time; there was coordination happening with the CIC (Controller in Charge); ground control; and ZZZ approach about an aircraft that had departed recently having inflight issues and will be returning to ZZZ. This caused me being distracted with a possible inbound aircraft. Additionally there were 3-4 aircraft conducting pattern work on the parallel runway XXL. At XA:32Z; Aircraft Y reported they were still in position on the runway; and that's when I saw Aircraft X on the runway and exiting downfield. Not actually seeing it; it appears that Aircraft X overflew the other aircraft in position and landed on the occupied runway. Aircraft X never called out about an aircraft occupying the runway. Recommendation: Due to ZZZ having a large amount of student pilot training; its not uncommon for pilots to be slow with readbacks or simply missing calls. Knowing this I should have been more focused with the readback and taken an extra moment to ensure the clearance was heard. I also failed to scan the runway.
