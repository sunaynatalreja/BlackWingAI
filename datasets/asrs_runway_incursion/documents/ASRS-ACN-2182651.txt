Synopsis:
First Officer reported difficulty in determining an assigned taxiway exit from a runway after landing; which resulted in the aircraft on final approach performing a missed approach. The First Officer suggested painting a runway exit line on the surface to aid in determining the runway exit to the taxiway.

Narrative:
After landing we were asked to exit at taxiway C; the taxiway exit is hard to see and no painted line showing the exit is properly displayed. We saw the taxiway C and started to enter when we realized that it was cover by lights. No damage was done to the aircraft or the light system. The airplane behind us had to go around which implicate a safety concern if is during night time operations.Suggestions: Best suggestion is to paint a line that leads you safely to exit the runway onto the taxiway. If during daylight and a little bit or rain; I can imagine that during night operations has to be very complicated to see the taxiway exit.
