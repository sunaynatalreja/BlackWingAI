Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported a ground conflict during taxiout at MIA airport resulting in an aircraft having to execute a go-around.

Narrative:
Contacted MIA ground for taxi. Controller advised expect Rwy 8L for takeoff and to taxi via Txy K to holding point for Rwy 8L.  We had loaded Rwy 8R during preflight; so during the taxi; I loaded Rwy 8L; entered the takeoff data; and confirmed waypoints.  The CA called for the Runway Change checklist and it was completed halfway to the holding point.  Ground then directed us to monitor Tower frequency approaching the holding point.  When tower contacted us; they directed us to cross runway 8L and hold short Rwy 8R and expedite for traffic on 5-mile final. As we crossed the hold short line for Rwy 8L; tower advised us to proceed onto hold for Rwy 8R.  I advised that we would need a few minutes to run checklists.  Tower replied with hold short Rwy 8L and the CA applied brakes; but we were past the hold short line by about 50 feet.  I advised tower and he said to then expedite and hold short Rwy 8R; traffic on 2 mile final. CA advanced throttles; but we did not clear Rwy 8L in time and traffic on final was directed to Go Around.  Holding Short of Rwy 8R we completed all the runway change items one last time and rest of departure was uneventful.Cause: Change of Runway assignment followed by expedited instructions to cross and then on to the new runway and hold before crew could run required checklists.  I also should have been more vocal about our requirements before attempting to cross Rwy 8L; but with the hold short instructions initially provided for the other side; I thought we would have time while holding short of Rwy 8R.Suggestions: When workload is high and expedited instructions are received to cross an active runway; request hold short of that runway to ensure all requirements are completed to prevent conflicts.

Secondary Narrative:
Our preflight was normal.  We had the aircraft set up for a departure off of runway 08R and also had the numbers for 08L.  We blocked out early.  Upon receiving initial Taxi clearance we were told to taxi via K to runway 08L and expect 08L for departure.  We immediately reconfigured the aircraft for 08L and had the runway change checklist completed before crossing taxiway K7.  Around approximately taxiway Z the ground controller had us switch to tower and monitor.  We were approximately abeam the beginning of taxiway K1 when the tower controller said 'Aircraft X; cross runway 08L hold short runway 08R traffic five miles out I need you to expedite.' We proceeded to cross 08L and were waiting to reconfigure for 08R until we were stopped and holding short of 08R.  All numbers and  power settings were exactly the same.  We had discussed and noted this before initial taxi. We just had to select runway 08R; reenter V speeds;  and repeat the runway change checklist. As we were crossing the runway 08L hold short line the tower controller said 'Aircraft X Line up and wait runway 08R.'  We responded we'd need a minute to change the numbers and run the checklist.  The controller then said 'Aircraft X hold short  runway 08L.' We responded 'Unable we have already crossed the hold short line.'  The tower controller then said 'Then I need you to expedite crossing 08L and hold short 08R traffic two mile final.' We had stopped when he initially told us to hold short and were now trying to expedite again. At that moment I immediately observed the aircraft on final begin their go around.Cause: When receiving our initial taxi clearance the controller initially gave us 08R then said 'hold on ….'  there was about a three to five second pause then he came back and said 'Taxi via K to runway 08L.'  I believe during that pause the ground controller likely checked with the tower controller or someone else and asked if it was ok to give us 08L. We did not ask for this however I think they were just trying to be accommodating. I think there was some sort of miscommunication between ground and tower and that translated into the tower controller giving us 08R when the ground controller had given us 08L.  In the moment the tower controller got audibly agitated with us as I felt he thought we weren't prepared.  In reality we were fully prepared for either runway and were set up for the runway given.  I just should have refused to cross runway 08L at the initial instruction until we were fully set up again.  In past experience I have also had them do almost the same thing previously to me. The previous time I stopped prior to 08L; reconfigured for 08R; then they eventually came back and reassigned us back to 08L prior to takeoff.Suggestions: In hindsight when the controller initially issued a runway different than we were expecting I should have just held short of runway 08L and told him we were going to have to change numbers and run the runway change checklist there.  I was trying to make it work in the wrong place.  Going forward I that's what I will do.
