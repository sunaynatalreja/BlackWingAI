Synopsis:
A pilot departing a non towered airport reported they observed a truck entering the runway at the same time they became airborne

Narrative:
The AWOS reported a temperature of 37 degrees Celsius; and a density altitude of 6300 feet. I have a personal limitation not to depart or land at an airport with a density altitude above 7000 feet in the Piper Tomahawk due to its limited performance. I communicated on the CTAF that I would be taxing for runway XX and performed all before-takeoff checks in the run-up area. A Cessna called on CTAF to announce it was on a 5 mile final. I announced I would be holding short of runway XX; and would plan to depart after traffic was clear of the runway. A few seconds later a King Air announces on CTAF that it will be overflying the runway to make left traffic for runway XX. I asked if they would be willing to extend their downwind; so that I could depart prior to their arrival without causing a traffic conflict. I mentioned how warm it was on the ground; and they agreed to build space in their pattern for me. Once the Cessna landed and called that they were clear of runway 29; I announced over the CTAF that I would be departing runway XX. I scanned for traffic on final; and for potential traffic conflicts on the 2 intersecting runways. Although the tall grass partially obscured the runways; it seemed clear. I began my takeoff roll; and noted how high the ground speed was before the airspeed was 'alive;' which I expected due the density altitude. I noticed two white pick-up trucks on a perpendicular taxiway; and assumed they would be holding short. I glanced inside to see I had achieved Vr; and when I looked outside to rotate; one of the pick-up trucks had started to cross the runway. Once I had realized what was happening; I was airborne and continued my takeoff. My best guess is that I rotated 2000' before the truck. The King Air; now on final called; 'hey ops; next time-if you're going to cross a runway; make a call. We have an airplane taking off and traffic on final!' The pick-up trucks had never made any calls on CTAF.I believe the heat; and my attention to the degraded performance of my plane due to density altitude narrowed my situation awareness. Had I widened my scan; I may have been able to identify the traffic conflict earlier; and rejected the takeoff. However; at the speed I was traveling when I did see the truck; I believe the best course of action was to fly the airplane away from the truck(which is what I did). The truck crossing a runway without communicating on CTAF was irresponsible and dangerous. Going forward I will be vigilant of not just other air traffic; but also ground vehicles. I was aware that non-towered airports may have air traffic that does not communicate using CTAF; but now I am aware that even ground vehicles may not participate in using the CTAF. I have learned from this experience to widen the scope of what threats to identify at non-towered airports.
