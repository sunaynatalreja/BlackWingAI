Synopsis:
GA pilot reported being on short final and an aircraft took off in front of the reporter's aircraft resulting in the landing pilot having to switch runways at F45 Airport.

Narrative:
Due to wind; departure and landing operations were conducted on Runway 14. 3 aircraft were in the pattern (RH) executing T/G (Touch and Goes). As I was on final for Runway 14 I announced myself final for T/G Runway 14; another aircraft at the holding point announced he would enter Runway 14 and depart. I calmly advised I was now short final for that runway. The answer was 'I'll be off when you arrive; I'm outta here'.The separation being now minimal (5 seconds to flare estimated); I sidestepped to the right of the runway and executed a go around (advising on frequency) as the traffic departed only slightly ahead of me. That traffic did not respond; and just went his merry way.This pilot just disregarded the rules of the air and put himself and others at risk.I wanted to report this runway incursion; in the hope to help raise awareness about reckless behavior.
