Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported taking the runway for departure with an airplane on short final. Reporter stated the other aircraft was not communicating its intentions.

Narrative:
Takeoff RWY 35 BDN. As the aircraft departing ahead became airborne; and visually clearing the final approach course; I crossed the runway hold short line and announced on CTAF; 'Taking RWY 35 for westbound departure.' Approximately 30 feet after crossing the hold short line another aircraft announced on CTAF; 'We're on short final.' I came to a complete stop; other aircraft announced going around. On CTAF I advised we'd heard no radio call and did not observe the other aircraft. Other aircraft responded; 'I made three calls.' There was also some mention on frequency of a 'dogleg final.' I never saw the other aircraft until it was established on downwind after its go around. In my aircraft there were three (3) pilots; with a cumulative total of 35000+ hours.  All of us were monitoring CTAF and no one heard a call from the other aircraft.Suggested remedies: Although radios are not required at non-towered airports; good procedure would be to make a call on CTAF/UNICOM and then pause for possible responses BEFORE crossing the hold short onto the runway. At non-towered airports; with other traffic observed on the ground and in the pattern; aircraft should fly a standard pattern so that they can be observed when on final approach.
