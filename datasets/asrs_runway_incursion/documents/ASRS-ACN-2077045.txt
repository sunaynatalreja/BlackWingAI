Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported conflicting ATC instructions regarding clearance to land while aircraft is on runway.

Narrative:
For performance reasons; we needed to use Rwy 20 instead of the landing runway of 02.  We put in our request for that with Ground Control and they cleared us to taxi to Taxiway Uniform; short of west taxi.  We held there as directed.  After an aircraft landed on Runway 02; we were cleared by Ground Control to Taxi onto Runway 02; contact tower at the end."  We back taxied to the departure end of runway 20 and as we turned to line up; we heard "Aircraft Y; cleared to land runway 02."  We rolled out to see an aircraft on final for runway 02.  Tower then announced; "Aircraft Y; prepare for go around; aircraft on runway."  They were sent around and the tower instructed us to clear the runway onto the west taxi area that was open.  We were never instructed to either clear the runway or line up and wait when ground control cleared us onto the runway.  It seemed as if ground control and tower had not fully coordinated the handoff."
