Synopsis:
A flight instructor reported a NMAC while taking off from a non towered airport with an aircraft departing opposite direction.

Narrative:
An instrument student with a PPL and I were heading out for a cross country from our base ZZZ to ZZZ1 in a Vans RV-12. The other plane involved in this near miss was Aircraft Y. During the engine warm up after start my student noted that he thought Aircraft Y had perhaps landed on RWY XX at ZZZ. I surmised to my student that the 'Aircraft Y' had only back taxied on XX as we had just checked the AWOS and winds were reporting 210 at 7 Knots. The winds and traffic had been using runway XY. On top of this the wind sock looked 80% full straight down the pike for runway XY. We taxied to a runup area on the ramp and I noted another Aircraft Z in the pattern for runway XY. As we taxied on 1 we heard again Aircraft Z in the pattern for XY performing a Power Off 180. The timing of our taxi worked out that when we reached the departure end of XY we only waited briefly for the traffic to clear the runway. My student made the traffic call 'Aircraft X departing Runway XY left closed traffic departing off downwind'. We taxied onto runway XY and powered up. The runway has an elevated portion in the middle of it; as we accelerated and lifted off at the crest of the hill I then saw an airplane at the departure end of XX; which seemed to be moving. I immediately assumed controls and verbally transferred to me; I continued the climb and side stepped to the left as it seemed the airplane on the ground was drifting off centerline to the right. A quick scan of ADSB gave me his tail number and I sternly asked on the CTAF 'Aircraft Y are you up?!' The pilot of Aircraft Y responded. 'This is Aircraft Y...'Me 'Did you make that call that you were taking off?'Aircraft Y 'I did make a call that we saying we were taking off...'Me 'We did not hear that!'Me 'Did you hear us a call that we were taking off?'Aircraft Y 'No we did not hear you'Me 'What in the world?!'Other pilots in the area chimed in that they didn't hear either call.Aircraft Y 'OMG well sorry about that if I heard you were taking off I wouldn't have taken off'Me 'Also the winds are right down the pike.. on XY'Aircraft Y 'Oh I just saw Aircraft Z had landed; taxi I though he came in on XX'Me 'No; he just came in on a power off 180 for XY; we called our taxi and take-off...well all is well that ends well'On subsequent review of the the audio it was clear that we had both made the appropriate radio calls. And from my perspective there was very little either party could have done to mitigate the CTAF and miss-communication. Aircraft Y elected to take off downwind. The hill in the middle of the runway played a factor of us not hearing each others calls. In the future any Aircraft Y taxiing to their ramp area I will query when rwy XY is active.
