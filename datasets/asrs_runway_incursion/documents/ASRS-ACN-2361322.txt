Synopsis:
PA-28 pilot reported a conflict with a stationary glider during takeoff from a non-towered airport requiring evasive action.

Narrative:
A glider was parked on south end of runway near the east edge. It was not visible from the north end due to its low profile and slight hump in runway. I made several radio calls on CTAF for taxi; holding short while performing run up; holding short again; entering runway to line up and wait; and departing. I heard the Cessna behind me on the taxiway; number two for takeoff; making normal radio calls. Nothing was heard from the glider or a tow plane. Shortly after liftoff; I saw the parked glider and immediately side-stepped to the right. I continued my climb and departed the area to the east. I would suggest that one person in the glider ground team have a handheld radio; monitor CTAF; and respond keeping others apprised of glider intentions.
