Synopsis:
Pilot reported a NMAC with an aircraft not under ATC control while being vectored for runway 9 at TIX. Pilot mentioned misunderstanding after clearing runway between ATC and pilot.

Narrative:
I was being vectored for RWY 9 at TIX.  I had a visual as it looked like ATC (Orlando) was oversaturated and going to send me past final.  I make the call to Orlando approach that I had the field  and received no response.  I finally made contact after a second attempt but I was already crossing the final approach course.  As I was cleared for the visual i had to over correct for the late vector by maybe 10 degrees in order to get established for the visual approach.  As I was on final I received a TA RA to climb.  I disconnected the autopilot but had the training Aircraft Y passing off my left wing by maybe 500 feet and radar showed -100 below.  I had flew the rest of the approach with no incident.  I asked tower if they had been talking to the training Aircraft Y and they responded they had not.  I told them I had received a conflict but had a visual resolution.  After landing tower told me to turn right on runway 18 and contact ground.  After exiting runway 9 and turning right onto runway 18 I contacted ground.  After several attempts I was able to get a response.  They asked why I kept moving on a closed runway.  I told them I had been told to turn right on runway 18 and contact ground which I had done.  They told me I was supposed to stop and contact ground.  No NOTAMs showed RWY 18 had been closed and as far as I knew it was still an active runway and I made my was to taxiway echo to come to a stop once cleared of 18.  I don't recall seeing a runway clearance line on RWY 18 showing I had cleared RWY 9.  I debated sending in this report but safety is my absolute number one priority and I want to be the best aviator I can be.
