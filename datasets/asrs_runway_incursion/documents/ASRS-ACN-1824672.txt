Synopsis:
Pilot Examiner reported a runway incursion at OMN airport and cited worn hold short lines as a contributing factor.

Narrative:
The hold short markings at the approach end of Runway 27 are blacked almost all the way out due to dirt or other material (actually look as though they have blanked out).Applicant wasn't sure if it was the actual hold short line.  Looking at the lines I wasn't sure those were the hold short lines; due to the lack of distinction; I looked to see if there were any closer to runway and didn't see any so knew these should be them.Applicant turned aircraft around quickly once he figured it out and the wing may have crossed the line. There were no communications from the Tower or Ground controllers about the aircraft position.Recommend that the hold short lines be cleaned or repainted as needed so as to prevent confusion as to the distinction of the lines.
