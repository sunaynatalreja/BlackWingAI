Synopsis:
Captain reported they stopped taxiing across a runway ATC had cleared them to cross to avoid a potential collision with a truck.

Narrative:
On the morning in question; we were taxing out from the ramp via taxiway 1 to runway XX. Were told to hold short on runway XY/XZ. When ground advised us 'clear to cross' monitor tower on other side; I was sitting on the right seat giving IOE to a PIOE in left seat. He stated his call out Clear Left Clear to Cross" and when I looked to my right; I saw a vehicle approaching down the center of the runway XY. The hold short bars are quite a distance from the actual runway; and we had not started moving when I called to stop. Had we proceeded there might have been a collision but unlikely due to our slow speed we had even moved and the distance of the hold short bars. Once the vehicle passed; we continued and made mention to ground but got no reply. Upon switching to tower on other side; we heard the tower talking to the vehicle about continuing down runway XY to across XX. Again; no mention was made. It is now obvious tower and ground were not communicating with each other. Our SOPs prevented this incident; but the ZZZ tower needs to be addressed."
