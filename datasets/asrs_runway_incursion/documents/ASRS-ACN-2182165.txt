Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported while holding short of a runway the aircraft nose was over the hold line resulting in ATC directing a go-around for landing traffic.

Narrative:
We were assigned a late runway change from the approach control; XXL to  vectors for a visual approach XXR. After Exiting runway XXR at 1; the clearance to cross XXL was not clear. We came to a stop and we reconfirm the taxi clearance to cross runway XXL and discover the nose is slightly beyond the line.The tower advised the landing traffic ( 1-2 mile final) to go around and notified us to contact the tower via phone call. The captain contacted the tower and provided the additional information. We debriefed the scenario using our tools.
