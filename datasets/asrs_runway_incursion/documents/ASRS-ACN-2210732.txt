Synopsis:
General aviation instructor pilot reported a near miss after landing with another aircraft attempting to land opposite direction on the same runway; during a training flight at a non-towered airport. The landing aircraft performed a go-around and flew over the instructor's aircraft at low altitude; then returned for landing.

Narrative:
Aircraft X was returning to ZZZ after completing a training flight over the beach. Immediately after touchdown on runway XX; Aircraft Y was seen coming head on with my student and I attempting a landing on runway XY. After trying to contact Aircraft Y (XXX.X) with no response; he appeared to conduct a go around (approx 20'-50' AGL) while side stepping slightly to the right on runway XX. Aircraft Y proceeded to continue straight off the departure end and made a steep left teardrop entry into final for runway XX. I kept trying to contact the pilot after clearing runway XX but did not receive a response until he was already taxiing behind me. After securing my aircraft I walked up to the Aircraft Y pilot to talk about what just occurred; and he admitted to be 'on the wrong frequency and is late to a meeting with his wife'.
