Synopsis:
Air carrier crew reported a conflict while on final approach with another aircraft that was on a takeoff roll. The flight had received a landing clearance but the crew felt there was not adequate spacing for landing and performed a go-around. They then returned to land.

Narrative:
We were flying the river visual runway 19 approach into the DCA airport. where we asked to fly the approach at 160 knots for the FO's first time flying the approach; after switching to tower they cleared us to land. Where we were fully configured before 1000 feet. Around 500 feet we noticed an aircraft was on the runway and noted around 400 feet. He was taking off and was only at 1000 foot marker. Where I Annnounced go round at which we performed to go around to give the river visual 19 another attempt. On our second attempt at the river visual runway 19 tower made a remark saying that next time that should've been enough space to land.Cause: Tower did not leave enough space between the departing aircraft and landing aircraft. This space between the two airplanes caused the judgment of the landing aircraft (us) to believe we had enough space to land on a runway that did not have another plane on it.Suggestion: Better spacing between aircraft.

Secondary Narrative:
We were flying the visual 19 into DCA. We had asked approach if we can slow to 160 sooner so that we get configured and stable early since it was my first time flying it. We were switched to tower and tower cleared us to land. As I started my turn to the right to line up with the runway we noticed an aircraft on the runway. At about 400 feet that aircraft was still around the 1000 foot markers and that is when we called a go around. As we called the go around tower asked us why we had to go around. We informed them that we did a go around because of the aircraft on the runway to which he told us that there would have been enough spacing.Cause: Aircraft was still on the runway when we were approaching short final. We did not think the departing aircraft would have been off the runway before us touching down.Suggestion: Better spacing between the aircraft.
