Synopsis:
Flight Instructor with student reported during takeoff an aircraft taxied onto the opposite end of the runway for departure; requiring evasive action.

Narrative:
My student and I taxied to runway 05 because the winds were out of 033. While performing run-up; I heard another aircraft call 'taxi to 23 from ramp'. I immediately said that there was an aircraft holding short of 5. When my student finished run-up; I call traffic again asking was there traffic taxiing to 23. The aircraft responded with twin is taxiing to 23. I advised him that we were holding short of 5 and would be headed out of the area so we wouldn't be in his way. After a brief inquiry from my student about not being able to see him on ADS-B; I said he was communicating with us so we should be fine. My student then called taking runway 5 for departure to the NW. As we started moving the other aircraft started to display on our ADS-B. As my student went full throttle I saw the traffic was positioned on 23. I called there was traffic rolling 5. Knowing he was a twin; I took the controls from my student; got us in a better configuration to get off the ground; and immediately starting going right off the runway path when I got in the air. After a short spat on the radio; we both went our separate ways.
