Synopsis:
A320 pilot reported a steering malfunction after landing rollout while taxiing on a taxiway. The pilot regained control of the aircraft; then continued to parking.

Narrative:
After Captain (CA) landed Runway XX at ZZZ he began exiting Taxiway 1. After established on 1; steering issues began and the airplane was steering left and right abruptly. CA used counter steering and counter braking to keep the aircraft on the centerline. Due to the short distance of clearing Runway XX and Holding Short XY; CA was able to get the aircraft under control inside of the hold short line of XY. Ground advised us to stop; which we complied with. Then told us to continue to parking via Taxiway 2.
