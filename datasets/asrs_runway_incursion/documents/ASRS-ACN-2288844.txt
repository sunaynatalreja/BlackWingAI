Synopsis:
A300 Captain reported after landing they observed a Cessna on their assigned taxiway past the hold short line and continued to the next taxiway..

Narrative:
Approach and landing to ZZZ was normal. On check in told tower our parking location and told either 1 exit or right turn off further down the runway if we couldnt make 1. At approximately 60 knots we transferred the controls to me and I began to maneuver to exit taxiway 1 on the left. It was about the time that I was starting my turn that I realized there was a Cessna approximately five feet from the edge of the runway on 1 and well past the hold short line for runway XX.  I ceased the turn and continued down the runway for a right turn off and informed tower of the airplanes position past the line. Tower provided a phone number to discuss our observation of what had occurred and whether we had to take evasive action which we did not. Contributing factors were construction at the airport; and the color of the Cessna which blended in with the pavement and the construction equipment in the background. I was looking at 1 as we were rolling out and never saw him until I was about to turn. I am not sure he would have been visible from the tower either due to everything around that particular intersection.
