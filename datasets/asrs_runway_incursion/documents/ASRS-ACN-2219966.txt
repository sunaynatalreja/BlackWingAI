Synopsis:
Air taxi Captain reported crossing a LAHSO line at HPN after following the yellow taxi lines and having to perform a tight turn. The reporter's larger aircraft did not have the turn radius to properly complete this turn.

Narrative:
Landed HPN XA:36 dropping off 13 passengers turn to ZZZZ. Crew picked up ATIS Runway 16 for departure HPN - ZZZZ. Taxi clearance did not specify runway in use or change of active runway. Taxi clearance XC:01 taxi Fox; Kilo; Lima. FO advised Aircraft X holding short Runway 11/29 on Lima. Aircraft X cleared taxi right on Runway 11 and hold short Runway 34. Followed yellow taxi line to hold short Runway 34 and in tight right turn Aircraft X crossed LAHSO line on Runway 11. Pavement markings (no lights) not visible until turn completed. Aircraft X is large and turning radius was too tight to meet clearance with no lighted markers.How to mitigate this risk from re-occurrence: Marker night lights should be added to LAHSO hold short position from Runway 11 to hold short Runway 34. Given the tight turning radius required; this specific taxi instruction and hold short clearance cannot be accommodated by larger; longer aircraft such as Aircraft X.
