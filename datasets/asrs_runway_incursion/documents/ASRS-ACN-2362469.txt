Synopsis:
Cessna 172 pilot reported a NMAC with a helicopter on final approach at a non-towered airport. The helicopter landed in front of the pilot on the same runway; no damage occurred.

Narrative:
There has been a good deal of helicopter training here over the last few years. Generally we never have a problem with the helicopters; as they try to account for the normal traffic here and are rarely a problem. Mind you; I'm not necessarily blaming the helicopter here; just trying to give some background about our particular airport.I made the usual radio calls at 10 and 5 miles; and I was following a Cirrus in about as closely as prudent. There was a helicopter making right traffic above us; and he seemed to be a new guy; but we were all making the appropriate calls. Because of the fact that I was hundreds of feet lower; had clearly announced final at a mile and a half out; and the helicopters are usually good about waiting for us to pass; I thought all was well. But he did descend fairly quickly; and landed on our end of the runway instead of the far end at about the same time I did.I thought it might be unwise to go around; since a helicopter could do almost anything. I always land at the start of the runway in a short distance -- since I always make the first turn-off -- so I did that; but did get a nasty comment from a pilot flying overhead. I don't think he was particularly blaming me. But I didn't like how it played out really; and I said at the time that it was probably my fault to mollify him.If you go by who is in front; well yes; he's a helicopter and he turned in front of me. If you go by who is lower; then I was. I think that I should have gotten a direct answer from him instead of just assuming that he was waiting for us both to land; but I was working with the Cirrus so closely on the radio that I didn't think he would try to get in between us.The safety lesson? Be careful around students? I'm not entirely sure; since I'm still thinking about it. I have always held that the helicopters have just as much of a right to be here as we do; but then again; they've been very considerate too. In this case; I probably figured that I had made radio calls and they would do as usual; and they probably figured that since they had made previous radio calls; it was all good.
