Synopsis:
A Flight Instructor reported that a student on a solo flight taxied onto a runway without clearance at the same time another aircraft was taking off.

Narrative:
My student was sent out on a solo into the practice area out of ZZZ airport in a C-172. All went well during the flight and he returned back to land at ZZZ. After landing on runway XX; he exited the runway onto taxiway 1 and was told to taxi via taxiway 2 and hold short runway XY. After reading back the instructions he accidentally taxied onto runway XY while a C-17 was departing runway XZ. Luckily the C-17 was able to stop in time and not hit the Cessna on the runway. My student exited the runway and was given towers number to call after the flight.

Secondary Narrative:
Told to hold short of runway XY and was unfamiliar with that side of the airport.  Was looking for runway XY and realized I was crossing it as a plane was taking off. The brakes and got out of the way of the plane and no damage occurred to any individual or aircraft.
