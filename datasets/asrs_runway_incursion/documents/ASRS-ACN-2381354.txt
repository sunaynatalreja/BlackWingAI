Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported they were cleared by ATC to cross a runway that had traffic on takeoff. Upon seeing the traffic; pilot stopped past hold short line.

Narrative:
I was cleared to land on runway 30L from the left downwind. I made a normal approach and landing and exited on taxiway B and was told to hold short 30R. I sat there for about 5 minutes due to lots of departures. Then I got a clearance to cross 30R on bravo and contact ground 121.7. I added power and looked left and saw it was clear and then looked right and Aircraft Y was on the takeoff roll on 30R. I was slightly past the short line and I stopped. Aircraft Y rotated and started to climb out and I heard the tower controller say my call sign and stop. I was stopped and then waited as Aircraft Y climbed out. I then queried ATC to cross 30R on B and I was given that clearance. The rest of the taxi to the FBO was uneventful. I was glad I cleared both directions and stopped before I heard ATC. A different voice came on afterward so I expect that there was training going on. BJC is an incredibly busy airport with lots of training. It reiterates how important it is to keep your eyes outside of the cockpit and make sure it is clear before crossing.
