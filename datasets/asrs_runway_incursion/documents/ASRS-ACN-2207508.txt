Synopsis:
Fractional flight crew reported being in the wrong location to hold short of LUK Runway 21 due to the airport's non-standard configuration for the hold-short line. The hold-short line for Runway 21 was located on Runway 25 and aircraft would have to stay on the latter runway to hold short of the former. The flight crew stated there were no notes in the taxi diagrams or charts providing this information.

Narrative:
Taxi Intersection A - C closed at LUK requiring back-taxi on Runway 25. Ground instruction were; Turn right on Runway 25; back-taxi to the end; hold short Runway 21; contact Tower." Upon reaching the end; we turned right exiting Runway 25; cleared the hold-short lines; stopped short of the next set of hold-short lines. Only then to realize the hold-short lines were opposite of normal. You would have had to remain ON Runway 25 in order to hold short Runway 21 and vice versa. There are lots of other airports and runways with short hold lines between two closely spaced runways. All others I have ever seen in my career do not have you hold on a runway to hold short of the other runway. This note is not in the taxi diagram hot spot notes; nor company notes; or supplemental chart pages on Jeppesen.Suggestions: Please add to hot spot notes."

Secondary Narrative:
Taxi Intersection A - C closed at LUK. Ground required us to back-taxi the full length of Runway 25 then hold short Runway 21. We completed the back-taxi and exited Runway 25 into the space between Runway 25 and 21 in order to be clear of both runways. It was then that we noticed that the hold-short line for Runway 21 was in a non-standard location and is actually located on Runway 25. This seems unsafe since there is enough room for an aircraft between the two runways; and the runways are similar headings which increases the chance of an aircraft landing on the incorrect runway. I do feel that the location we ended up at while holding short of 21 is actually safer than where you are apparently supposed to hold short.Suggestions: The hold-short line should be moved off of Runway 25; and the hot spot area description should be updated to note the non-standard location of the hold-short line; since that is not discussed.
