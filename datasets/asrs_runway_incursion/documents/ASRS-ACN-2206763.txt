Synopsis:
Light aircraft reported that after takeoff from TCY he became aware another aircraft broke off their landing attempt because of the conflict with his departure.

Narrative:
This is my first report. I was distracted by cabin conversation and 'get-there-itis' when preparing for takeoff from TCY yesterday afternoon. As this was just a quick fuel stop; I was eager to get back in the air and failed to do a full run-up. On taxi; I listened to CTAF and do not recall any traffic coming in on downwind; base; or final; though the cabin was not sterile at the time. After I made a radio call noting that I was taking the active Runway 30 for a straight out departure. That's exactly what I did. I only found out after takeoff at 800 feet on upwind that there may have been a plane on final and they aborted their landing because of me. I'm not certain that this actually happened as I never saw the plane on final; my passenger in the back seat said he thinks that that's what happened. I did see another aircraft taking off right behind me and it could have just been him on upwind behind me. Regardless; I thought I would report it and in the future; I am going to endeavor to have a sterile cockpit on near the runup/end of runway and to do a full runup and prep for takeoff every flight.
