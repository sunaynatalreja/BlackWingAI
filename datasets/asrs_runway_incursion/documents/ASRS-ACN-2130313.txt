Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported a traffic conflict when receiving a takeoff clearance with another aircraft on final approach. The crew did not accept the clearance and remained short of the runway to avoid the conflict.

Narrative:
While holding short of Runway XX (ZZZ) at taxiway 1 for departure ; ATC tower clears our flight for takeoff. Both the Captain and myself are aware and have in sight a company aircraft on short final for runway XX about 1 mile from touchdown. I acknowledge ATC and tell them we are unable to accept the takeoff clearance due to landing traffic. Our takeoff clearance was never cancelled however we remained short of runway XX. After the aircraft lands we are given another takeoff clearance which we accept. On takeoff roll; the controller then clears the aircraft behind us for takeoff which they do not accept due to our aircraft still being on the runway. We debriefed the situation as a crew

Secondary Narrative:
We were holding short of Runway XX at ZZZ for takeoff. ZZZ Tower cleared us for takeoff when there was a company aircraft still on short final. My First Officer did the right thing and did not accept the takeoff clearance due to the Company aircraft on final. Someone not sure who got on the radio after we didn't accept the clearance and said good decision. After that another person in the radio who I assume was one of the pilots landing the plane also said; Yes thank you." After the Company aircraft landed we received a clearance to line up and wait and we accepted it. I proceeded to line up and wait. After the Company aircraft cleared the runway down the field; Tower cleared a different Company aircraft for takeoff on Runway XX. That company aircraft did not accept the clearance explaining there was another plane on the runway; referring to us; we were still on the runway. After that radio call Tower cleared us for takeoff. We departed and continued the flight without any issues. Just want crews to be aware of this so they can be extra vigilant when getting a takeoff clearance. Make sure to always check both sides of the runway before accepting any crossing; line up and wait; or takeoff clearance."
