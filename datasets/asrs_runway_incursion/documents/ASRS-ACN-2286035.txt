Synopsis:
C-172 pilot reported another aircraft overflew him and landed while cleared he was beginning his takeoff roll on the same runway.

Narrative:
ZZZ AirportI was holding short of RWY XX at 1 for IFR release. This entire time; my nose was angled to view the approach path of landing traffic; that combined with paying attention to the radio traffic for situational awareness.The airport was getting busy; with notable comments from the controller; and other pilots discussing air traffic conditions. Eventually; we were given our takeoff clearance; and begin to roll onto the runway. I already completed my before takeoff clearance; and specifically remember my readback to ZZZ Tower. As I was aligning with the runway and gradually setting takeoff power; a Cessna 172 flew above me and landed on the runway.After 3-4 seconds of processing what I was seeing; I looked at my hand and noticed my throttle was about 50-75% set; and confirming that a plane indeed landed in right front of me; and is now stopping/slowing down; I rejected the takeoff and notified ATC that I was rejecting.From my memory; I don't recall anyone saying anything for a little bit on frequency until both me and the other C172 were closer to each other (at this point slowed down to taxi speeds); and then I was instructed to make a left on 2. I verified with Tower that I was issued a takeoff clearance; and tower confirmed that I was given a clearance to initiate takeoff.After being given instructions to taxi back to RWY XX; I asked for a phone number to debrief with tower.
