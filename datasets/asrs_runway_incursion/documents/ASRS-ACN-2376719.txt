Synopsis:
Shorts-D330 First Officer reported obscured and limited view of final resulted in a runway incursion and critical ground conflict with traffic on short final.

Narrative:
During IOE with a new captain; holding short of RWY XX; we did not have an aircraft on a 1 mile final in sight due to the sun and our relative position to the RWY. We heard comms from an aircraft on the RNAV XX practice approach about 3 miles away. We called 'lining up and departing rwy XX'; and after about 30 seconds we listened an aircraft calling Going around off rwy XX; as we were retracting the gear about 50 ft off the ground. We verified a vertical separation of about 500 ft. with the conflicting traffic. The aircraft never warned us they were on short final to prevent us from lining up on the rwy.
