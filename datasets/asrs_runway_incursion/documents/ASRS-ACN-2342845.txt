Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported crossing the DWH Runway 17L hold short line without clearance; stating that the hold short marking was faded and not parallel to the runway but perpendicular and offset an unusual amount in proximity to the runway.

Narrative:
I was leaving DWH at approximately XA:00 local time; the weather was clear and the field and controllers were very busy. Earlier in the day; I arrived on an instrument flight plan earlier in the day and landed 17R which is the larger more utilized runway. The taxiways upon exiting that runway are very intuitive and standard. When I was preparing for my departure I was given 17L; which is a smaller less utilized runway. The taxiways are non-standard and poorly marked compared to 17R. I was given taxiing instructions for 17L and the incident occurred at HS6 on the airport diagram. I was instructed to hold short of 17L at the hold short line and made an error and did not stop.Two factors affected that error; 1. I was anticipating the hold short line marking to have been parallel to the runway and it was in fact perpendicular; and offset an unusual amount in proximity to the runway; and 2. the marking on the runway were faded compared to the markings on 17R; in my opinion. The Ground Controller told me to stop and I complied. Tower Controller suggested that I call the FBO to mention that the markings were faded as Tower Controller reported they had had an uptick in similar incidents. I did not call the FBO.
