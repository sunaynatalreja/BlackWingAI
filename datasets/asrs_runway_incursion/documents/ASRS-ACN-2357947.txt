Synopsis:
Air carrier Captain reported a traffic coordination conflict on final approach when ATC cleared a heavy transport for takeoff on an intersecting runway at night. The Captain could not locate the departing traffic and confirm separation; then executed a go-around to de-conflict from the traffic.

Narrative:
While we were less than 1000 feet off the ground ATC cleared Aircraft Y for take off from runway 15R. The ATIS did not say anything about using 15R only landing 04R and departing 9 with winds 320 at 05 knots. We were not told when we checked in about traffic holding 9n multiple intersecting runways nor were we expecting 15R to be in use with a tailwind.  After Aircraft Y was  cleared for takeoff both me and my FO questioned each other where the airplane was where the intersection of the two runways were; and how this would work with our location to the runway. I queried ATC about them clearing some one to take off with us that close and ATC said yes they were cleared. Having never seen this operation in over a decade of operating in Boston and mixed with a voice I have never heard working the tower in Boston before I told the FO I don't like this and he agreed. With it being night and our location to the threshold of 15 R we could not see the departing traffic and I told the FO let's go around. He agreed it was bad and we initiated a go around. ATC seemed surprised we were going around but mixed with the surprise of a departure from a runway we were not expecting in an unknown operation with ATC's less than stellar recent performance throughout the country recently we were not going to hang around and see if it would work. We went around then landed shortly there after with no issues.
