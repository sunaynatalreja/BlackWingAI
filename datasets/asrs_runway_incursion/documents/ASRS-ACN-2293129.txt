Synopsis:
Air carrier pilot reported an aircraft parked on a taxiway blocked the air carrier from exiting the runway after landing causing an aircraft on final approach to go around.

Narrative:
After landing 17 in KOA; on roll out; we were instructed to take exit Alpha 6 at the end. There was an aircraft [Aircraft Y] on approach for 17 in sequence behind us. We then heard tower confirm with a parked aircraft that had landed prior to us; that they were clear of taxiway Alpha 6 and parked at the 'hammerhead'. We heard that aircraft confirm that they were 'clear of Alpha; parked at the hammerhead'; they were however not parked in the hammerhead and instead were parked on taxiway Alpha 6 completely blocking us from exiting the runway. We immediately notified tower of this and our inability to exit the runway. Tower immediately issued a go around to the aircraft on final for runway 17 behind us. We offered to do a '180' to back taxi runway 17. Tower cleared us to 180 back taxi 17 and exit A5; Alpha to parking" we complied and taxied to parking without further incident."
