Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported taking off opposite direction of landing traffic and taking evasive action to avoid collision.

Narrative:
A friend and I flew to ZZZ; announced and landed. ZZZ has a 3500 foot paved runway on a golf course with homes on this air park. Large pine trees 30 to 40 feet high are located on both sides on the runway. Calm winds were prevailing; but neglecting to announce; we departed Runway XX. As we cleared the trees and about halfway down the runway; I noticed an aircraft turning onto final for Runway XY. I immediately turned 90 degrees to the left to escape the danger. Following the established procedure of announcing our position; altitude and intentions at uncontrolled airports would have taken care of the problem here; which should have included listening for others.
