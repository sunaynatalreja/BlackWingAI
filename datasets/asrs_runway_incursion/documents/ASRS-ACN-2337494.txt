Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported during takeoff roll; an aircraft landed in front of them; resulting in rejected takeoff.

Narrative:
School aircraft landing as I completed run up near hold short line. Plane landed and was rolling out halfway down runway (short runway); heard call out on downwind for a second school aircraft; saw aircraft on downwind directly in front at pattern altitude. I called out intentions for line up and wait; departing to the east of the downwind departure. Sat at end of runway until aircraft cleared at end; immediately called out my departure and began to accelerate when a plane landed directly in front of me past the displaced threshold; I aborted the takeoff; pulled power and turned off the runway to taxi back.
