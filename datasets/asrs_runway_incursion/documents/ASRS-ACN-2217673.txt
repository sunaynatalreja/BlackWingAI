Synopsis:
C150 Flight Instructor reported landing as a succeeding C172 landed approximately 1;000 feet behind them resulted in taking expedited action to resolve a critical ground conflict.

Narrative:
During pattern work at ZZZ; my aircraft; took off and completed a pattern closely followed by a  Cessna 172. The 172 turned downwind so tight behind us that I asked if they had us in sight. They confirmed they did; then proceeded to follow us through the downwind with less than 1/4 nm separation. We turned base; the 172 called extending the downwind and called turning base 10-15 seconds after we did. They called final while we were short final and upon confirming the student maintained control on rollout; I turned to see the 172 touch down about 1000 ft behind us; at which point I instructed an accelerated exit from the runway; which the student complied with immediately. I confirmed the tail number as they exited behind us.
