Synopsis:
Pilatus PC12 Captain reported ground conflict while on runway with aircraft on final approach resulting in a missed approach.

Narrative:
ZZZ TOWER cleared Aircraft X for takeoff Runway XX sandwiched in-between two GA aircraft; one on departure roll; and one on left base.  ZZZ TOWER subsequently provided conflicting choices to Aircraft X while on the runway.  Aircraft X PIC assessed executing takeoff was the better of the choices provided and elected to execute the takeoff.Upon lining up on Runway XX; Aircraft X PIC observed departing GA traffic turning initially right; then reversing back left across centerline.  ZZZ TOWER then instructed Aircraft X to either immediately exit Runway XX or start takeoff roll.  Aircraft X PIC subsequently assessed upwind traffic was clear of centerline and tracking further away; set takeoff power; and reported 'Aircraft X rolling.'  GA traffic behind Aircraft X made decision to go around.  Aircraft X was unable to report reason for hesitation on the runway due to frequency congestion.  No further interaction between Aircraft X and ZZZ TOWER; other than a frequency change.Contributing Factors: Aircraft X PIC hasty decision to select the most efficient / fastest option for clearing the runway when presented with an urgent unexpected choice by ATC.Aircraft X PIC failure to deliberately consider the most conservative option when given an urgent unexpected choice by ATC.Aircraft X PIC implicit assumption that Second in Command concurred with decision to set takeoff power (which was later revealed to be a correct assumption).High density airport traffic environment.Constrained maneuvering airspace due to terrain.ATC urgency to depart Aircraft X for unknown reasons.Aircraft X PIC inability to see and assess departure traffic until on the runway.Departure traffic erratic maneuvering across departure corridor. Unknown distance between Aircraft X and following traffic once on the runway.Corrective Actions: Aircraft X PIC continue to delay takeoff decision until factor traffic identified and assessed.Aircraft X PIC consider most conservative response more deliberately when given an unexpected choice by ATC.Aircraft X PIC seek explicit crew concurrence when confronted with an unexpected decision.
