Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported two near misses at a non-towered airport. The pilot performed a go around to avoid the other aircraft taking off; then another evasive maneuver in the go around climb to avoid the aircraft again once it became airborne.

Narrative:
Landing at CLW.  Reported downwind and base turn number 2 behind uninvolved aircraft.  Rolling out on final; observed subject aircraft taking the runway for departure.  Over the radio I reported that I was 1 mile final.  The other pilot said 'sorry I will get out of the way.'  I thought that meant taxiing to the next available exit from the runway.  Aircraft started rolling and continued the takeoff after the first aircraft cleared the runway.  I reported and executed a go around.  Offset from the runway and climbed.  Since I am faster I caught up with the other aircraft.  That aircraft then turned right toward me with a right turn.  I expected her to turn crosswind to the left and I would follow at a safe distance.  When she turned toward me I transmitted 'I can't believe you turned toward me!'  She said sorry about that and continued in my direction.  I pulled back on the stick to gain vertical clearance and avoided the other aircraft.  That aircraft continued the right turn and later landed at ZZZ1.
