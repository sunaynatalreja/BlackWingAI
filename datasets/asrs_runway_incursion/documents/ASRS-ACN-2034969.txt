Synopsis:
Pilot flying Beech v35 aircraft reported exiting onto a runway instead of taxiway per clearance resulting in a go-around by another aircraft on approach.

Narrative:
Landing Runway XX at ZZZ upon landing advised to exit via Taxiway 1. I exited Runway X/XY the right hand turn before taxiway. A jet was landing behind me so I was unable to re-enter Runway XX to exit. I held short Runway XX. Aircraft Y went around and I re entered Runway XX and exited on taxiway without incident to FBO. In a single pilot task saturated environment and a landing Jet it may have been better to go around and not be rushed to exit runway. In order to avoid the GO around ATC could have advised Taxiway without interference. Thank you.
