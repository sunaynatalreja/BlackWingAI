Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported a conflict while landing with FOD placed near the runway threshold to identify the runway as closed by NOTAM at a non-towered airport.

Narrative:
I conducted a routine flight form my home base (ZZZ) to (3N6) Old Bridge airport. As part of my routine flight preparation; I failed this day to do a complete check of NOTAMs when reviewing the airport information before takeoff. Upon what seemed a normal arrival and announcement into the airport pattern; I could see some localized work being conducted next to the T hangers at the far end of the runway. The runway was clear; unobstructed; and did not appear to have visible markings. Approach was normal and without event. In preparation to flare as I crossed over the runway end I observed what I first believed to be a cover/possible aircraft cover of sorts wrapped and balled up near the end of the asphalt near the grass edge. With the intent to land and report the debris near the field; I continued. The entire runway was clear and unobstructed. As I was beginning my flare crossing the threshold; I overserved sandbags and quickly realized the airport was likely closed and the debris was likely a fabric X placed originally on the threshold. Given the current wind conditions at the time; (10/12 gusting to 18/20) and the short field I felt the safest course of action was to land and immediately stop as opposed to aborting the landing. Once stopped I shut down and walked down to make contact with personal working to advise them of the condition of the temporary airport marking as well as to arrange to restart and taxi safely to the ramp which were both conducted without incident.
