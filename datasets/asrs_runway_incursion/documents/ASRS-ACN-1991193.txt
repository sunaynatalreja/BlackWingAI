Synopsis:
Pilot reported inadvertently crossing the hold short lines onto a taxiway after being unable to recognize the faded and unmaintained taxiway markings on the pavement at CXY airport.

Narrative:
I was pulling out from the maintenance area to taxi to the FBO. I didn't see the hold short line for the taxiway C. It must have been very faint as this part of the airport pavement looks older and not as well maintained as the rest of the field. I made my call to ground control to get permission to taxi from the ramp to the FBO. The FBO is on the opposite side of the airport. I was then informed that I had already proceeded onto taxiway C. I didn't see a sign for entry onto C and the line was not visible or visible enough.
