Synopsis:
B737 flight crew reported observing a B777 under tow crossing the runway departure end on landing rollout; which resulted in a critical ground conflict.

Narrative:
On the rollout after landing I noticed the back half of a 777 being towed across the active runway on what appeared to be taxiway 1. It wasn't apparent if the aircraft had a beacon on but he surely did not have any strobes on. I queried ATC about the aircraft on the runway and he said that he was clearing and to not worry about it. The FO and I do not recall hearing any clearance by tower to an aircraft to taxi across the active runway nor did we see them due to the lack of strobes and nighttime conditions. If I saw this aircraft halfway out on the rollout that means the whole body was across the runway during the touchdown.

Secondary Narrative:
Following conduct of ILS XXR app into ZZZ in night VMC conditions; after safely landing on the runway; and upon rollout vicinity of intersection 1; the CA and FO identified a wide body aircraft taxiing across the end of RWY XXR; appearing to be on intersection 2 northbound. At first identification of the aircraft; it appeared its rear half was still on XXR exiting.  The aircraft appeared to have minimal lighting on during its crossing of XXR.  Tower was advised of the aircraft identified by the CA as we identified it and they replied that it was exiting now. There was no prior announcement by the tower of the crossing traffic.
