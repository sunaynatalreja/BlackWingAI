Synopsis:
Airport operations personnel reported aircraft overflew ground personnel performing maintenance on the runway.

Narrative:
ZZZ issued NOTAMs for a closed RWY with a 10 minute Prior Permission Required (PPR) on Unicom due to Work in Process (WIP) painting operations taking place throughout the day. Maintenance was on the RWY XX threshold actively painting when the airport operation center; who monitors the Unicom frequency and issues PPRs; noticed Aircraft X lined up for final on RWY XX. Airport operations staff attempted on [multiple] different occasions to reach Aircraft X on [Unicom] to inform them of the PPR and the presence of maintenance crews on the runway. Airport staff then attempted to reach Aircraft X on guard [frequency] and [the] frequency for surrounding airports with still no response. At the same time; airport staff informed maintenance to clear the runway immediately for a non-responsive aircraft on short final. Aircraft X passed within 100 feet of RWY elevation on what turned into a low approach over the top of maintenance crews who were unable to clear the runway in time as the aircraft passed over them. ZZZ Operations attempted to contact the pilot. Given the proximity of the aircraft to maintenance personnel with a RWY closure for WIP; the lack of communications by Aircraft X; and the concern to airport staff safety; the airport believes this incident constitutes an incursion by the pilot of Aircraft X.
