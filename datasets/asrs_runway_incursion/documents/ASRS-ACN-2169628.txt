Synopsis:
Air Carrier Captain reported during final approach the Tower Controller cleared a jet to take off and an aircraft to cross the runway prior to the Captain landing; creating extremely close spacing. On short final approach Tower directed a go-around.

Narrative:
On approach into Boston I wanna say close to 1-2 mile final and near the 1;000' (I'm not 100% on the distance/alt) call they cleared a jet for take off. The FO and I were shocked as it was close so we clarified our landing clearance and was told cleared to land. During the 500' call we noticed a regional jet taxiing close to the runway then entered the crossing Taxiway E. I believe; we were on 33L and they taxiied onto the runway on E I believe. We were shocked as this was extremely close and as we were getting ready to initiate a MAP (Missed Approach Procedure) we were told to go around. Seems like both the jet and crossing traffic were way too close and the Tower should have waited. This makes it hard for crews as our red eye was delayed.
