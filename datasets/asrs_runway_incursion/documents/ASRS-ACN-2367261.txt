Synopsis:
Air carrier First Officer reported a runway incursion after confusing an ATC clearance to line up and wait after arriving aircraft.

Narrative:
Flight number: ABCD / ZZZ-ZZZZAt XA:07; ZZZ parking position was 1. Taxi clearance was received from Tower to taxi via 1 across Runway XX and continue via 2-3 to Runway XX. (Clearance from the tower was obtained to taxi via 1 across Runway XX and continue along 2-3.) The aircraft taxied out via 1-2-3 to Runway XX.At XA:14; the tower cleared the preceding aircraft for takeoff.At XA:16; the tower instructed: 'Aircraft X; cleared Runway XX line up and wait after arrival.'At approximately XA:15 at ZZZ; Aircraft Y was on short final for Runway XX.As CM2; I read back: 'Line up and wait Runway XX; Aircraft X.' Approximately one second later; I added: 'Line up behind; Aircraft X.' No correction or clarification was provided by ATC following either readback at the time.In a dynamic runway environment at ZZZ Runway XX; the crew's actions; including runway entry during the rollout phase; were based on real-time interpretation of the received transmission and a visual scan of the runway environment.After establishing position on the Runway XX centerline; the preceding aircraft had not yet cleared the runway. Tower then instructed the subsequent arriving aircraft to go around and advised that our runway entry was not authorized. We were subsequently instructed to vacate Runway XX via Taxiway 4 at ZZZ. The event was subsequently classified as a runway incursion.Contributing Factors (Human Factors)Contributing factors included ambiguity in the timing structure of the clearance phraseology ('line up and wait after arrival') in the context of operations on Runway XX at ZZZ; requiring real-time interpretation in a dynamic runway environment.VHF communication characteristics and associated audio conditions may have resulted in perceived audio degradation and reduced intelligibility of the transmission. Post-event review of recorded ATC communications was conducted using a high-fidelity audio playback system with slowed playback to enhance intelligibility. This review suggested that portions of the transmission may have been subject to audio degradation during high cockpit workload conditions; resulting in reduced transmission intelligibility.The flight crew independently perceived the transmission in real time as a Line Up and Wait-related clearance.The absence of ATC correction to two separate readbacks may have reinforced the crew's understanding of the transmission.At the ZZZ Runway XX 3 intersection; runway guard lights were available as the primary visual cue; and no runway stop bar system was present. The available lighting configuration provided limited additional visual confirmation or redundancy for runway entry verification between ATC and flight crews. This may have reduced an additional layer of defence that could otherwise assist in resolving ambiguity or reinforcing runway entry status during high-tempo operations.Safety Actions TakenContinuous traffic monitoring was maintained prior to runway entry at ZZZ Runway XX. No evasive maneuver was required during the event; and no collision or loss of separation occurred.Corrective Action / PreventionIn future operations at ZZZ; greater emphasis will be placed on positive confirmation of runway status prior to entering a runway under LUAW clearances; particularly when sequencing is referenced in conjunction with arriving traffic.Enhanced cross-checking of ATC instructions between crew members will be reinforced to mitigate potential interpretation divergence. Additional awareness will be maintained regarding VHF communication degradation and the importance of seeking explicit clarification when any ambiguity exists in runway-related clearances.
