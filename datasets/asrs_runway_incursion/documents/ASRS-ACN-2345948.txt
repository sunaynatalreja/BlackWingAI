Synopsis:
B737 flight crew reported crossing hold short line during taxi for takeoff. Crew continued taxi per ATC instructions.

Narrative:
Clearance by tower to taxi to runway XX via 1 and 2 and I understood the instructions to be to backtrack to holding point of runway XX and hold short of runway XX at that point; a common taxi instruction to runway XX. I am very familiar with ZZZZ and have been doing ZZZZ turns all month. I read back taxi instructions to the FO 1; 2; backtrack XX and hold short. Obviously there was a miscommunication. We completed the before takeoff checklist to the line before taxiing to try and avoid threats and distractions. I then called for 'final items' which I will never do again in this particular area because it brought the FO heads down at a critical point. I would say the majority of the time I finish the entire before takeoff checklist before taxiing out of ZZZZ due to this particular threat. Just as we crossed the hold short line tower told us to stop which we did but nose of aircraft was across the hold short line. There was an aircraft on final that was sent around; guessing approximately 1000-1500 feet. I did clear left and right and saw no aircraft before proceeding across hold short line.

Secondary Narrative:
We did 1st portion of 'before takeoff checklist' before taxi. Got clearance to taxi to 'holding point XX via 1-2'. We start the short taxi and the captain calls final items. I perform the finals and while reading the checklist the tower calls us to stop and hold position. I now realize we have crossed the hold short line. Captain misunderstood the directions thinking we were cleared to back taxi; but we were only cleared to hold short due to an aircraft on arrival.
