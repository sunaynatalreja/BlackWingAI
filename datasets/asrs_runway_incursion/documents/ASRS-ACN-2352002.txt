Synopsis:
Small aircraft pilot reported a conflict with an aircraft on approach while waiting to depart. Pilot elected to delay the takeoff.

Narrative:
At OBE; numerous flight training aircraft were performing tight circuit touch and go's resulting in aircraft on the ground being unable to depart or even cross runways. This continued for some time.My Aircraft was #2 for departure waiting as training aircraft performed several complete circuits of Touch and Go's.I keyed the mic contacting Aircraft Y (#1 for take off) and suggested that he may need to do a expedited take off as the pattern was crowded AND I suggested 'OBE traffic extend their downwind or practice some go arounds'.Someone in the pattern also repeated my request. Pattern Aircraft did extend there downwind; spacing was then provided for Aircraft Y to safely depart with a expedited departure.My Aircraft was now #1 for departure RWY 5  and I waited for the next landing aircraft to clear. I saw the next aircraft turning base to Final and I announced and took RWY 5 for departure.  As I entered the runway for takeoff someone on the radio yelled 'GET THE [EXPLETIVE] OFF THE RUNWAY' and at that instant I also got a ADS-B Traffic Alert 0.4 Mile.Those two things caused me doubt my situational awareness and location of other aircraft. I decided the safest thing was to not move until I was certain it was safe to depart.This delay on the runway was not planned malice; rather shaken from the belligerent radio call and traffic alert; I needed time to sort out the situation before moving for takeoff. The almost simultaneous traffic alert and unexpected belligerent radio call was a massive distraction causing me to pause and re-access while still on the ground (RWY 5). Apparently the landing traffic was determined to land.Most of the flight training aircraft in the area operate in the patterns Professional; courteously and Safely; yet there are some exceptions.
