Synopsis:
CE550 pilot reported during takeoff roll an aircraft that had just landed was still on the runway. Flight crew climbed over the aircraft.

Narrative:
After a twin prop had landed we were cleared to line up and wait. The other aircraft was told to exit on the taxiway and shortly after we were cleared for takeoff. We proceeded with a normal takeoff and as we reached Vr and began to rotate we noticed the prior aircraft was still on the runway at the far end. We rotated and climbed normally and cleared the other aircraft by approximately 200ft AGL. We notified Tower who confirmed and then transferred us to departure. No further communication communications or issues occurred with the incident.
