Synopsis:
Flight instructor and student pilot reported the student was given a hold short instruction and accidentally entered the runway. Another aircraft on final approach had to perform a go-around and ATC directed the student to turn back to taxiway.

Narrative:
My Student readback the hold short instruction; but then proceeded onto the runway. Taxi clearance and route had been briefed; as well as ATC ground instructions at towered airports. Student had several hours of experience at other towered airports; as well as several trips to ZZZ. An aircraft was on short final and had to perform a go-around due to runway incursion. It appears as though the student was simply confused; and read back correctly; but then proceeded to take the runway. We have debriefed the incident and gone over the correct course of action that should have been taken; as well as ways to prevent this happening in the future.
