Synopsis:
Gulfstream Captain departing a non towered airport reported they entered the runway to depart and observed another aircraft taking off on the opposite direction runway which rotated before them.

Narrative:
We were at FBO and the First Officer announced on Unicom we were taxiing to Runway XX for takeoff to the North. Short of the Runway the First Officer called for takeoff clearance. After getting cleared ZZZ the First Officer announced we were entering Runway XX for take off. Another aircraft said something (I did not understand) and then 'I can't stop.' I stopped my aircraft as our nose entered the Runway to see a twin engine prop aircraft taking off towards us on Runway XY. It rotated before our position and climbed as it passed us. If they made a call stating they were taking off it happened while we were listening to ZZZ Center or we missed it.
