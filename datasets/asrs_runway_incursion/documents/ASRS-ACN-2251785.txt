Synopsis:
General aviation instructor reported a traffic conflict while on final approach with another aircraft performing a takeoff at a non-towered airport during a training flight. The instructors aircraft performed a go around to avoid the departing aircraft; and returned for a landing.

Narrative:
At approximately XA:30; Aircraft X was on short final approach to land on Runway 36 at CBF. The aircraft made its calls as appropriate on the CTAF frequency; as shortly before it reached the runway threshold; Aircraft Y took to the runway to take off. Aircraft X immediately initiated a go-around and performed a go-around. Aircraft Y soon took off before Aircraft X reached the Runway 18 threshold; and Aircraft Y departed to the east. Operations continued safely and Aircraft X came back for a landing.
