Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported during takeoff roll an aircraft crossed the active runway; resulting in a rejected takeoff.

Narrative:
While taxi from the ramp at Marfa (MRF) Wind was favoring RWY 22 (wind 190 @8). I announced taxi using taxiway Alpha to back taxi 22 to run-up area. Upon completions of runup I announced lining up on RWY22 to depart to the south. I saw Aircraft Y taxi before making the call for departure. It seemed like it stopped to which i assumed them holding short for my departure. As I increased throttle for takeoff I noticed the aircraft moving onto the runway. I then aborted take off which left me around the 1;000' mark. I observed the aircraft continue to taxi on Alpha. Once cleared from runway I then proceeded with full throttle to commence takeoff. There was never any taxi announcements or advisories from the aircraft from taxi to line up and take off. The departed from RWY 13 shortly after my departure.
