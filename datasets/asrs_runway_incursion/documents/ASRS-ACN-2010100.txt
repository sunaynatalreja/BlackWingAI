Synopsis:
Air carrier First Officer reported a taxiway and runway incursion at ORD and the need for a hot spot designation at the intersection of taxiways A2 and G.

Narrative:
We called for taxi and ground told us Runway 9C/FF via A; A2; cross 4L; G; hold short R. Captain and I both confirmed the taxi with one another. We understood the route; clearance to cross; and the hold short. We had confusion as we were taxiing on A2 as it was merging with G and 4L. We mistakenly followed the yellow runway exit line. We both started to realize we were seeing runway markings perpendicular to us rather than taxi line. Captain was already slowing the plane down; but by that time we were over the hold short line. By this point; Ground cleared us across 9R and we got a new route via E. A unfamiliar airport; busy and complicated; both pilots relatively new - especially in Chicago. Lack of hot spot. We were looking for traffic as we were crossing 4L. This needs to be marked on the 20-9 page as a hot spot. Especially since the Controller told us over the phone that many pilots get confused in this exact location.
