Synopsis:
Air carrier First Officer reported a traffic conflict on final approach when another aircraft on the runway did not start their takeoff roll when cleared for takeoff. ATC issued a go-around clearance to the aircraft on final; then returned for another approach and landing.

Narrative:
We were inside the FAF for a visual approach to runway 36R into CLT. After being cleared to land tower issued a takeoff clearance to an aircraft holding in position on the runway. The aircraft did not respond to the clearance nor several subsequent transmissions. The captain and I noticed this and mentioned to each other to prepare for a go around. At approximately 800 feet ATC issued us a go around. We completed the go around profile and followed tower instructions to be vectored for another approach and landed without incident.
