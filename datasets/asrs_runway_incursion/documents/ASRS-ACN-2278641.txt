Synopsis:
SR20 student pilot reported an engine shutdown after landing and was stopped on the runway at a non-towered airport.  Another pilot helped move the student's aircraft off the runway; onto the ramp.

Narrative:
Airplane started up in ZZZ1 normal and did RunUp without any problems. Flight from ZZZ1 to ZZZ at 5500' was uneventful. Approach and Traffic Pattern in ZZZ was normal; I followed normal SOP's due to DA (Density Altitude) under 5000' Mixture was at full during landing. After touchdown and braking I put a small amount of power back in to taxi to the next exit of the Runway. I don't recall if the engine RPM started to drop before or after i put the power back in; but the engine just shut down. I was stranded on the Runway with no engine; did a traffic call so no one lands during the time I was on the runway. I tried to restart it several times with normal engine start list; engine start when hot list and engine start when flooded list; tried on both tanks as well; but the engine didn't restart. As I was pretty much at the beginning of the runway another plane in the pattern decided to land in opposite direction and taxied on the Twy 1 over where I was on the Runway. The PIC of the other plane then helped me to push the aircraft of the Rwy and Twy onto the nearby Ramp to make the airport usable again.
