Synopsis:
Pilot reported an experimental aircraft; not on CTAF; that taxied onto the runway resulting in a ground conflict.

Narrative:
I was pic of Aircraft X while upon landing and flare out; Aircraft Y crossed taxiway D & Runway 6 at COE. The aircraft in question did not announce that he was crossing Runway 6 @ D at COE while I was landing. I announced I was inbound for full stop landing Runway 6 at 10 miles out; 5 miles out; and short final as I was on the ILS Runway 6. As touching down; that is when the aircraft crossed. I landed on the 1000-foot mark. I did not go around because i was already stopping and had plenty of time to slow down. If I were to go around; my ground roll would have caused a close call or possibly crashing into the aircraft if I had not taken off in time. I believe that Coeur d Alene airport is in need of runway guard lights as well as a control tower as this airspace gets a lot of traffic and have multiple close calls here.
