Synopsis:
General aviation pilot reported a near miss when another aircraft entered the runway for takeoff while the pilot was on short final at a non-towered airport. The pilot performed a go around and landed safely.

Narrative:
I was on final for Runway 6 at KBUY after doing a normal VFR pattern entry. I announced all my position reports on CTAF. I did extend my downwind leg by 2NM due to many aircraft in the pattern. As I was on short final; an unknown aircraft entered the runway to take off prompting me to go around. I maintained visual separation; completed the normal pattern entry; and landed safely without any issue. What I believed caused the issue was that the pilot did not see me on approach and thought it was safe to enter the runway. I cannot remember if the person was on CTAF. However; I do not remember the pilot saying any intentions for takeoff.
