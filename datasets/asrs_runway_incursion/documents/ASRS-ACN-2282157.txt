Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported high speed rejected takeoff at SKBO after ATC cancelled their takeoff clearance when a landing aircraft failed to clear the runway.

Narrative:
After receiving takeoff clearance from tower; we were instructed to reject the takeoff.   We executed the rejected takeoff procedures; vacated the runway; and completed the appropriate checklists; including reviewing the brake cooling charts. Dispatch was advised of the rejected takeoff; and a maintenance entry was made in the logbook. Cause: The aircraft that landed before our takeoff clearance had vacated the runway; but its tail was still beyond the hold-short line. As a result; tower instructed us to reject the takeoff.

Secondary Narrative:
We were operating flight ABCD from SKBO to ZZZ. We taxied to runway 14R and were holding short of the runway. There was an [air carrier] airplane that had just landed on runway 14R. Tower then gave us a lineup and wait clearance and as we got in position they issued us a takeoff clearance.We started our takeoff roll and at around 100 kias tower directed us to reject the takeoff. We followed tower instructions and rejected the takeoff according to our procedures; vacated the runway and completed the appropriate checklists; including reviewing the brake cooling charts.Dispatch was advised of the rejected takeoff and a maintenance entry was made in the logbook.We then coordinated with Bogota operations to find us a gate to return to. Upon reaching the gate maintenance was there waiting for us to inspect the airplane.We asked tower the reason for the directed reject takeoff and they mentioned that the [air carrier] who had just landed on 14R took the high speed on K5 but they had issues with the ground control frequency and ended up not completely clearing the runway hold short lines.Cause: There is no way for pilots to see runway hold short lines at a intersection almost 8000 feet down the runway. We saw that the runway was clear but we could not see that part of their tail is over a hold short lines that is angled towards the taxiway. Tower should only issue takeoff clearances once the airplane is completely clear of the runway.
