Synopsis:
Air carrier Captain reported a conflict with an aircraft slowly taxiing on their landing runway at BOS airport resulting in a go-around.

Narrative:
We were on final for RNAV 4L into BOS. As we were going through the before landing checklist; two air carrier planes were given permission to cross to get to the departure runway (9). They were told to expedite as we were on a 3 mile final.I heard it through the headset; but missed where the Air carrier X plane was crossing. The FO (PF) and I (PM) looked ahead and saw Air Carrier Y crossing at Charlie. We could not see Air carrier X on either side of the runway.As we got to around 500 ft; I saw Air carrier X taxiing slowly along runway 14 and it looked like we were going to go right over the top at a close distance. I verbally announced that it didn't look right and called for the Go Around.On the go around we advised ATC that there was an airplane crossing below and that was the reason for the go around. We were resequenced and landed with no other issues.
