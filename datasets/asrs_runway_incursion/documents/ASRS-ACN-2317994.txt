Synopsis:
Reporter expressed concern with gate security procedures and unauthorized access to the airport surfaces at JPX airport.

Narrative:
This airport has two gated locations where passengers would be flying in and out of. On this date; Person A had arrived at the main terminal but was instructed to drive over to the Executive Terminal after the staff had verified that they were picking up Person B. Person A had been given the airport code to get through the gates which is the same code for all gates at the airport. Person A did not follow the instructions to drive to the other terminal but instead entered the nearest gate and decided to use the active runway to drive over to the location they were looking for. An aircraft landed on that same runway 10 minutes later. Urgent recommendation to install more signs on all airport gates; to change all gate codes and to have those codes be made available to authorized personnel only.
