Synopsis:
A Navion pilot reported a loss of control after starting engine due to engine stuck at full power resulted in runway and taxiway incursions.

Narrative:
Flood starting my aircraft engine on the ramp about 90 degrees to the runway . Engine started with full power; I could not reduce the power . With brakes on - accelerated toward the taxiway and runway . Crossed the taxiway; the infield; and onto the runway . I was able to kill the engine with the mixture control and roll off the runway at an intersection. This even happened very fast . I was first concerned with stopping; unable to do so I concerned myself with steering away from taxiway and runway lights. On the runway;I killed the engine with mixture and was able to roll off the runway.My mechanic and I will be checking for any binding or sticking on all engine controls to prevent this from ever happening again.
