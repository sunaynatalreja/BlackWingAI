Synopsis:
Cessna 340 pilot departing a non-towered airport reported an aircraft entered the runway and began its takeoff behind them before they were airborne.

Narrative:
After witnessing a near miss involving 2 aircraft landing at ZZZ. Aircraft Y; which in my opinion initiated the near miss incident a few minutes prior quickly exited the runway and got in line directly behind my aircraft.  I was waiting for landing and departing aircraft.  I was number 3 in line on Taxiway 1 waiting for departure runway XX.  When it became my turn; I waited for departing aircraft to turn and entered the runway.  I immediately began my takeoff roll and made radio calls.  I immediately noticed that the Cessna [Aircraft Y] behind me had entered the runway during my roll and began his own roll before I became airborne.  I felt that this action was aggressive and dangerous.  Aircraft Y is an aircraft used for instruction and a student and an instructor were in the plane.  Just a few minutes prior; this same aircraft was involved in a near miss.  I exclaimed on the radio that Aircraft Y was wrong and I feel that this aggression on takeoff to begin to roll and take the runway before I became airborne was a result this near miss being witnessed by me.  I was piloting a twin Cessna and have a witness who was sitting right seat with me and saw both events. I felt that the actions taken by the instructor were unsafe and if he did have a student pilot then it is abhorrent to place a student in those situations for landing and takeoff.
