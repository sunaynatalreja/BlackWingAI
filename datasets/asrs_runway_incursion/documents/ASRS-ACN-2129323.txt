Synopsis:
C182 pilot reported crossing active runway resulted in a corporate jet rejecting take off and a critical ground conflict.

Narrative:
While taxiing to the active runway I needed to cross the active runway. I stopped prior to the runway; looked toward the approach end; and announced my intention to cross. I did not notice the jet about 2500 feet away and after my announcement started to cross the runway. At this point the other pilot announced he was taking off.  I crossed as fast as I could to clear the runway and he aborted the takeoff. I don't believe we were closer than 1500 feet.During the incident I was distracted by the fact that I just got the plane back from its annual was concentrating on making sure everything on the plane was set properly and working. In addition; I had a passenger that was to my right and in my line of site. I did not hear anything on UNICOM prior to the incident. I had a shot time before changed to UNICOM from AWOS. Lessons learned.
