Synopsis:
Flight Instructor and solo student reported a conflict on takeoff; and that trees off the end of Runway 31 at 06A airport were obscuring the view and need to be trimmed.

Narrative:
Student pilot solo took off with an experimental airplane on final; prompting the experimental plane to go around and side step. Student explained that there was another plane on final first who he held short of runway 31 for. After that plane landed; he checked base to final but missed the experimental plane. Both planes on final had made radio calls but he mistook them for one airplane. Additionally; trees off the approach end of 31 significantly block the view of base to final. The experimental plane was also not displayed on ADS-B; which the student said he checked before takeoff. Some things that could prevent this in the future:Standard phraseology of reporting that you are #2 for landing at an un-towered field if someone else is on final ahead of youTrimming the tops of trees near the runway to aid in visibility of the base-to-final legIncreased adoption of ADS-B out
