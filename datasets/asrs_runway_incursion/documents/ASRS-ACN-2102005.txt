Synopsis:
A flight instructor reported they expedited their exit off the runway due to another aircraft landing for a touch and go 700 feet behind them.

Narrative:
We were completing an instrument instructional flight back to ZZZ airport. We entered the pattern at circling minimums after shooting the ILS XX into ZZZ airport. A Cessna 210 was departing when we were arriving and flew behind us in the pattern. When we were joining the downwind the other aircraft stated they had us in sight and remained behind. On the final; we stated we were on final and our intentions for a full stop landing. Once we landed; we rolled out and were about to turn to exit onto the taxiway when I saw the other aircraft landed behind us on the runway. I estimated the distance to be within 1;500 ft and expedited our exit of the runway. We exited the runway and called clear once past the hold short. The other pilot made no comment on landing while we were on the runway.

Secondary Narrative:
I was working with a student in the traffic pattern; and the C-210 was also in the pattern.  We were completing our last landing and had just turned final and made our radio call.  The C-210 was on a base leg close behind us.  We touched down on the 3rd runway stripe and had to proceed down the runway to get the exit.  Were about 500' from the exit when the C-210 called they were on short final.  As we were turning off the runway onto the taxiway; I turned to see where the C-210 was; they were accelerating on the runway to take off again; presumably doing a touch and go.  There was about an estimated 700' of separation while on the ground.
