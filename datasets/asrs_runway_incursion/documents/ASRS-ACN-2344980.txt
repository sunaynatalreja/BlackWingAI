Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported after being cleared for takeoff MRLB ATC canceled their takeoff clearance for traffic down field.

Narrative:
I was PF.  We were holding short of runway 07 waiting for takeoff while a Aircraft Y landed in front of us.  After they landed we were told to line up and wait.  The Aircraft Y appeared to clear the runway at the other end and we were cleared for takeoff.  Just after the FO made the '80kt' callout; tower told us 'Aircraft X cancel takeoff clearance.'  After a 1-2 second hesitation I initiated a rejected takeoff.  Asking tower for details on the problem we were told that the Aircraft Y never cleared the hold-short line at the other end.  We exited the runway and reviewed the rejected takeoff considerations.  Because it was >80kts an maintenance logbook entry was required.  I contacted dispatch and maintenance control who directed us to return to the gate for inspection.  After the inspection was complete and brake cooldown times were observed we departed again.Cause: Lack of taxiway and gate space in LIR leads to planes stacking up on the only 2 exits for runway 07.  The aircraft that landed did not have room to clear the runway.  Every takeoff also requires a back-taxi which is very often 'expedited' for traffic.  It seems like the airport does not have the ramp or taxiway space to support the number of operations.

Secondary Narrative:
On Day 0; I was pilot monitoring of Aircraft X as first officer with scheduled service from LIR to ZZZ. The captain and I arrived to the airport well-rested after a 23-hour overnight and the arrival to the airport and airplane was standard. There were no issues with any of the aircraft origination or checklists; and no anomalies with respect to preflight duties or clearance delivery/routing. We were given clearance for push back and start from gate XX; which was accomplished normally and we joined the line for taxi to the departure end of the runway. The airport had been on runway 07 operations for the entire time we were there; so a full-length back taxi was required. Upon reaching the runway; we waited in the turnaround for arriving traffic behind an aircraft. There was an arriving Aircraft X that landed a few minutes after the aircraft departed that; once past us; we were issued a line up and wait clearance; which we accepted. After waiting in position for approximately two minutes; we were given a takeoff clearance by the tower. Initial power up and acceleration was normal. I called 80" and almost immediately after; tower calls "Aircraft X; cancel takeoff clearance." The captain and I were a bit confused as there was nothing hazardous immediately apparent in front of us; but we initiated a rejected takeoff. The deceleration was smooth; and callouts were standard. Once stopped; I started a timer and dropped the flaps to 40° and the captain retracted the spoilers and made an announcement to the cabin to remain seated. Once slowed; we were asked if we could make a 180 on the runway to return to the departure end; which we were able to accommodate. I was instructed to perform an after landing flow; and the captain and I began to discuss the requirements for a rejected takeoff and brake cooling requirements from the table in the AOM. The auto brakes did not fire; so we knew we were below 90kt when the reject was initiated; however with the adjustments for the length of taxi and the headwind; we determined that the most conservative cooling time was 45.9 minutes. We were not in the caution or melt zone; so we set the parking brake when coming to a complete stop.When queried the reason for the rejected takeoff clearance; the tower responded that an airplane had not fully cleared the runway. They tried to issue us a second takeoff clearance while we were calculating cooling times and contacting dispatch and maintenance control; and ultimately we were instructed to return to the gate for a visual inspection. We were caught in a lot of traffic; but made our way back to the gate and maintenance was waiting on us. We elected to not give the opportunity to deplane given the efficiency with which we were parked and accommodated by maintenance. The airplane was determined to be safe to continue on; so we closed the main cabin door; and pushed back for a normal flight arriving to ZZZ about an hour and a half late. No injuries were reported and no further problems with the airplane were experienced. Cause: We were issued a late cancellation of takeoff clearance for a runway incursion that was impossible to see from the departure end of the runway. Flight and cabin crew followed SOPs to mitigate injury or escalation of the event."
