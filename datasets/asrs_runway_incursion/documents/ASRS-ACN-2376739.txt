Synopsis:
A Flight Instructor on short final at an non towered airport reported initiating a go around to avoid a departing aircraft.

Narrative:
I was in Aircraft X and was doing some pattern work with a student at ZZZ. We were very short final around 300' AGL; when Aircraft Y pulled out onto runway XX and took off. We asked him if he knew that Aircraft X was short final and he stated; 'Yes I'll be out of your way in a second.' With us approaching at 70KIAS and descending around 500fpm; I knew we would collide with him or land on a runway with traffic still present on it. So I made the decision to initiate a go-around. We side stepped to the left of runway XX since I also saw that Aircraft Y was continuing with his departure. We continued after that and rejoined the pattern and landed safely. To me a small bit of impulsivity and macho-ness were to blame for this.I think that Aircraft Y thought he had plenty of space being in a Cessna 182 than he actually did and; even when made aware of traffic that would have collided with him; continued his departure with this knowledge. I also think the pilot in Aircraft Y should have hesitated since he knew that us in Aircraft X were less than a mile away on final; and waited until after we landed to takeoff.
