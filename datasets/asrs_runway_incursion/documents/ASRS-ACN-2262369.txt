Synopsis:
Fractional First Officer reported a conflict with another aircraft landing while back taxiing on the same runway at a non-towered airport. The flight crew immediately stopped the aircraft; then cleared the runway after the conflict.

Narrative:
The initial plan was to proceed to ZZZ; due to high winds that exceed our limitations; it was determined that RIL was the alternate plan. After getting our clearance we proceed with vectors provided by ATC. We planned for RWY 8 which ATC continued to provided us vectors. Upon visual contact with the airport; we cancelled our IFR flight plan and proceeded in the approach. ATC informed us about the traffic in the area which included Aircraft Y behind us; we acknowledged and proceeded informing our position reports. As we were approaching the airport; we noticed that the aircraft was simply approaching us. We proceeded with the landing and during our backtracking on the runway due to an aircraft holding short of RWY 26; we noticed Aircraft Y had landed right behind us. We immediately stopped our maneuver backtaxiing the runway and contacted Aircraft Y which did not responded on the radio. We proceeded to clear the runway and No further action happened afterward.
