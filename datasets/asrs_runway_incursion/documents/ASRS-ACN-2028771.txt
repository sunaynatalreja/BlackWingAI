Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported the new runway hold short line at MKJS Airport; Montego Bay; Jamaica; is not depicted on the 10-9 chart nor are there any NOTAMs advising of the new hold line.

Narrative:
We were given taxi instructions to taxi to Runway 07 hold point via Taxiway Alpha. Had a clear uneventful taxi all the way to what we thought was the hold point for Runway 07. Held short completed our checklist and informed Tower we ready for departure. Next thing we heard from Tower was we crossed the hold point". Then a pause followed by cleared for takeoff. We both looked at each other at first not really understanding what he meant. Off the nose was the hold lines for Runway 07 but they have been what appears to be recently painted over. New hold lines were behind us. There were no NOTAMs as to hold point nor on the 10-9 airport chart was there any indications as to were any runway markings are located. We departed uneventfully."

Secondary Narrative:
Cleared to taxi to Runway 07 in MBJ via EA hold short. Approaching Runway 07 observed old hold short line by end of runway missing new hold line further back located before turn to runway.
