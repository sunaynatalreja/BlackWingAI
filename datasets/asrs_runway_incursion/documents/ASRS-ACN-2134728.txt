Synopsis:
Pilot taxiing from FBO at LUK mistakenly entered Runway 21. The reporter cited confusing signage as one of the factors.

Narrative:
On taxi out in light rain I missed the turn out of the FBO throat onto Taxiway A. The intersection is shared with Runway 21R. As familiar as I am with LUK and in spite of my years and time of experience I missed the correct turn and back taxied on 21R. I pulled off of the runway onto Taxiway G as soon as I realized my mistake. 21R was inactive at the time and there was no conflict. The signage out of the new FBO location was confusing-at least it appeared that way to me. And the prop wash/rain was a factor also. And although I had a chart out; it wasn't in my hand. Next time it will be.
