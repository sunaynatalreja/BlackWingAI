Synopsis:
An Experimental Biplane pilot; no radio; reported runway incursion when pilot failed to observe traffic on final; resulted in aircraft go around.

Narrative:
At approximately XA00 local time; at ZZZ at an uncontrolled field; I taxied out to the threshold of RWY XXL in biplane single place open cockpit with no Radio. Seeing [no] aircraft in the pattern or on final; I proceeded to take off. Unknown and unseen by myself; a Beech aircraft lowing four-place mono airplane was approaching to land on Rwy XXL. The Beech pilot initiated a go-around to an uneventful landing; announcing that a runway incursion had occurred. Aircraft separation was estimated as several hundred yards; and no damage was incurred by either Aircraft. Difficulty in seeing a blue and white aircraft with blue sky background; and the Aircraft was not flying the designated traffic Encourage all traffic to fly a designated flight pattern and maintain visual awareness and alert to all traffic.
