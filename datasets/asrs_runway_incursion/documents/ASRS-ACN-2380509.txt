Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported they were cleared to taxi across a runway then stopped just prior to entering the runway when they observed an aircraft on landing roll.

Narrative:
After landing on XXR during taxi to the gate; we were cleared to cross runway XXL on [taxiway] 1 and told to contact ground. We began crossing the hold short line and taxing onto the runway when we saw landing lights of oncoming traffic. We stopped short of the edge of the runway and switched back to tower to enquire about the traffic conflict. We were told that traffic was giving way to us and to continue our taxi across the runway. We cleared; contacted ground; and taxied to the gate uneventfully. We debriefed the event before the next leg but were still uncertain of the situation. We later looked up the recording to get a better understanding. It appears the oncoming traffic; a PC12 was cleared to land after we were cleared to cross. They were told to turn right at [taxiway] 2 and missed the turn; resulting in the near conflict. After we had been given the frequency change to ground; tower tried to call us back and advise that traffic was giving way to us.

Secondary Narrative:
After landing XXR and exiting at taxiway 1. We held short of XXL until being cleared to cross by ZZZ tower and told to contact ZZZ ground control. We began moving when I noticed the full suite of landing lights of a Pilatus pc12 at a fast rate of movement towards our left side. I stopped aircraft movement immediately and parked. At that point our aircraft was over the hold short line but our nose not having crossed the runway edge markings. The pc12 came to a stop on XXL between taxiway turns offs 1 and 2. We questioned tower and ground; and they told us the pilatus was waiting for us and in a frustrated and hurried tone (for the first time hearing it) told us the PC12 was waiting for us; and issued verbal instructions to continue crossing at taxiway 2. After reviewing recorded ATC instructions; the PC12 received landing clearance for XXL and on rollout; I believe tower told the pc12 to exit XXR with a right turn at 2. They taxied past 2 after being cleared to land on XXL. We were told to cross and contract ground. We switched frequencies and didn't hear the initial prompt that the pc12 was to wait for us. This became evident to tower when we switched back to tower frequency to query them after we entered this conflict closer to taxiway 1.
