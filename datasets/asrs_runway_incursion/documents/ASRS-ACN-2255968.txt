Synopsis:
Light aircraft pilot reported clearing a golf cart by about 40 feet after it drove onto the runway just before he took off at 1B2 airport.

Narrative:
On Day 0 at XA30z; after announcing intentions to takeoff runway 21 on the CTAF at Katama Airpark and having accelerated to ~5 knots below takeoff speed a golf cart entered runway 21 from the left side headed same direction (from the high-speed or ninety-degree intersecting taxiway). With enough airspeed I elected to liftoff; accelerate in ground effect and cleared the golf cart by ~40 feet. Once safely airborne I made an attempt to contact the driver on CTAF but was unsuccessful. I also attempted to contact the airport manager after safely on the ground following the flight but was equally unsuccessful; will reattempt.
