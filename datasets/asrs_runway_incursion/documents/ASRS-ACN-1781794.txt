Synopsis:
Air carrier flight crew reported misunderstanding GSO ATC taxi instructions resulting in a runway incursion.

Narrative:
I was acting as PIC (Pilot in Command) and PF (Pilot Flying). On taxi out from maintenance facility; ATC Instructions were 'cleared to taxi Mike; Alpha cleared to cross runway 14.'Just prior to reaching the intersection of Mike taxiway and runway 14 ATC Instructions heard was 'clear to cross runway 14' The PM (Pilot Monitoring) read back; 'cleared to cross runway 14.'At the point just past the hold short lines and the runway ATC asked if we were holding short. A short exchange was made between us and ATC as ATC stated we were to hold short. An aircraft was held in position on runway 14 and ATC instructed us to proceed and cross runway 14.This flight was an test flight as the day before we had a number 2 engine failure. During taxi out a series of checklist items are accomplished in addition to the normal SOP line flight checklist items. Due to the preceding day's events and root cause of the engine failure information obtained; I ran additional thrust settings changes while on taxi to induce multiple pneumatic pressure bumps and fuel flow oscillations to further test the engine performance. Both the PF and the PM have an abundance of items to accomplish during taxi out on a test flight; I heard two separate clearances to cross runway 14; repeated the clearance verbally to the PM while physically scanning the approach end to my left stating 'cleared to cross 14; clear on final.'While conducting test flights the 'normal everyday expected clearances' and communications exchanges with ATC are handled quickly while simultaneously running additional checks pertinent to specific tasks.

Secondary Narrative:
I was acting as the pilot monitoring on flight.  The day prior we had a block turn back due to an engine shut down during taxi out for the test flight.On an FCF flight taxi time is quite busy. We have many checks to perform; sometimes with very little taxi time to do it. Also; on this day we were very concerned that the engine was properly repaired and we were performing extra checks to make sure that we did not have an engine failure on the takeoff.We were told to taxi to runway 05R on Mike and cross runway 14.  I read back instructions to Taxi to runway 05 R on Taxiway Mike and Cross runway 14. As we were doing our taxi checks for the test flight I once again heard instructions to cross runway 14; and I again read back clear to cross runway 14. After we crossed the hold short line for runway 14 Ground Control instructed us to stop and asked if we had cross the hold short line for 14. I advised that we had and he had then told me that he gave us instructions to hold short Of runway 14. I advised him that I read back clearance to once again cross runway 14. There was an aircraft waiting to takeoff on runway 14. He advised us that we were now clear to cross 14 and contact Tower for takeoff on runway 05R.
