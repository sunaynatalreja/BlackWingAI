Synopsis:
A General Aviation pilot reported turning onto crossing Runway 29 instead of Taxiway H after landing on Runway 34 at HPN.

Narrative:
I had landed on Runway 34 at HPN. I have landed there before but basically am unfamiliar with the field. Tower instructed me to back taxi on Runway 34; right on Taxiway H and right on Taxiway K. By mistake when I turned off of Runway 34; I taxied onto Runway 29. When I realized my mistake I was approaching Taxiway L. I then turned left on Taxiway L and proceeded to Taxiway K. I should have known I was turning onto Runway 29 but the intersection was so wide (the junction of Runway 34; Taxiway H and Runway 29) that I didn't realize my mistake until I was closer to Taxiway L than I was to Taxiway H. I should have been more diligent to follow the ground chart that is in my aircraft. And I should have asked for progressive taxi instructions since it was a confusing airport and I was not familiar with it. Luckily; at that time of night no other plane was landing or taxiing so there was no immediate danger but I recognize the seriousness of unauthorized taxi onto a runway.
