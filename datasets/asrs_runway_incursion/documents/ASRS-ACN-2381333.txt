Synopsis:
Air taxi pilot reported ATC cleared them to line up and wait on a runway and then cleared an aircraft to take off from the same runway. Air taxi pilot attempted to exit runway before ATC recognized the conflict.

Narrative:
My aircraft was holding short of runway 10 on H intersection. ATC cleared Aircraft Y from the A intersection to proceed onto runway 10 for a full length taxi back of the runway. I was then instructed to proceed behind Aircraft Y to line up for 10 at the H intersection and wait. We waited roughly 10-20 seconds expecting our takeoff clearance. ATC then cleared Aircraft Y for takeoff on runway 10. Aircraft Y responded with the cleared for takeoff 10 response. I then realized the mistake and made a radio call roughly stating that my aircraft was on runway 10 at the H intersection on a line up at wait clearance. ATC did not acknowledge my radio call. ATC then restated to Aircraft Y that they were cleared for takeoff again runway 10. Aircraft Y again responded with a cleared for takeoff runway 10 response. I made another radio call to ATC and Aircraft Y that my aircraft was on runway 10 for a line up and wait clearance at H intersection again with more emphasis. I did not get a reply from ATC nor Aircraft Y on the runway. My co pilot who was pilot flying then released the brakes to prepare to depart the runway on the B taxiway ahead and to our left. My co pilot then jumped on the radio stating that our aircraft was on the active runway. We got a response from ATC telling Aircraft Y to standby and not a cancel takeoff clearance. Aircraft Y acknowledged the radio call. ATC then gave us cleared for takeoff runway 10 runway heading up to 6;000 ft. We read back the instruction and proceeded with our takeoff. There was a published NOTAM of ATC students engaged in on the job training. No supervisor intervened during the incident if this was a student controller. It was a very bad lack of awareness and proper phraseology for the controller and possibly Aircraft Y's pilot.
