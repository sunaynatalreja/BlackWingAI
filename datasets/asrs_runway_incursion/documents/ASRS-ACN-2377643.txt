Synopsis:
SR-22 pilot reported a runway incursion after landing when turning onto another runway instead of a cleared taxiway; resulting in an aircraft on final being directed to go-around by ATC to provide separation.

Narrative:
I completed an ILS approach to XX and when I landed I was waiting for Air traffic control to tell me where to turn off and it was silent. I did not hear anything so I said that I am going to the ramp and they said OK turn off on Taxiway 1 and I thought I saw the Taxiway 1 sign to my right so I turned to the right it turns out that I turned onto a runway so they told me to turn left onto taxiway 2 and I heard them tell another aircraft to go around. I thought I turned on Taxiway 1 on a taxiway. I did not realize that I inadvertently turned onto an active runway. The air traffic controller was very calm and just told me to turn onto the taxiway. I told the other aircraft to go around; which was uneventful. I have learned a huge lesson in this situation to study the taxiways in a lot more detail than I usually do and also to turn off at the earliest available taxiway. I was always in the habit of waiting for Air traffic control to ask me where I'm going and then tell me where to turn off but from now on I'm just going to turn off at the soonest available taxiway and then let air traffic control tell me what to do next. in other words contact ground; etc. I have learned my lesson and that will never happen again I think what could have helped is when I landed if they wanted me to cross runway XY then turn right on Taxiway 1 instead of saying turn right on Taxiway 1 they should have said cross runway XY then turn right on Taxiway 1 that could've helped my situational awareness.
