Synopsis:
CRJ900 flight crew reported a rejected takeoff when a B737 taxied onto the runway in their takeoff path.

Narrative:
Having taxied from the terminal to runway XXR under dark skies with mist and on damp surfaces; we were given departure instruction and our takeoff clearance: we were cleared to depart XXR with a left turn out upon reaching 400 ft to heading 350. The most noticeable feature of the runway has the high intensity centerline lighting. At approximately 80 knots; the first officer as pilot flying alerted us to the presence of an aircraft crossing at what I estimate was approximately 2000 feet down the runway and it was crossing our runway at taxiway 1. Once I grasped the magnitude of the situation; I called; reject; reject. My controls. The FO had already begun to decelerate and I took over the braking applying harder left brakes due to the increasing gap created by the Air Carrier X 737 crossing runway XXR from left to right. I can recall that upon my first noticing of this aircraft was only a silhouette forming and later noted there were no obvious navigation lights or anti-collision lights; which might have alerted us sooner.     We cleared the runway and were given instructions to continue taxiway 2 left on 3 and hold short of runway XY; but I stopped on taxiway 2 to set brakes and announce to the passengers to remain seated. I recall the controller asking us if we would like to try again or would we like to return to gate. We asked to hold somewhere to perform checklists and to cool the brakes. We continued the flight once adequate brake cooling had occurred and when agreed the aircraft and crew were performing nominally.0

Secondary Narrative:
Starting with receiving taxi clearance for runway XXR under the lower ceilings and cloudy skies. The CA and I had done proper checklist leading up to taxing and getting ready to take off. Receiving takeoff clearance turning left heading 350° at 400ft we further did the checklist and ultimately began the takeoff roll as normal. As first noticed the runway lights were at a very high intensity but both pilots engaged in focusing on callouts and taking off runway XXR. As I the pilot flying waiting on the callouts we had set thurst as normal but beginning to accelerate I had noticed a silhouette from a plane in the distance around the middle of the runway had started to cross. I didn't appear to see any lights on the aircraft crossing which could have aided in understanding the situation and making actions sooner. Approximately 2000 ft from the Air Carrier X aircraft crossing I had noticed and took action to reduce power and start braking while calling out plane in the distance. Shortly after reducing power and deacceleration the CA looked up and acknowledged the airplane crossing the runway and took controls while verbalizing reject reject; my controls.  As now reverse roles and pilot monitoring we had veered off to avoid wingtip strike to the 737 crossing. I was observing carefully and making sure we were clear of the airplane crossing. Shortly after; I made call to ATC as instructed by the CA to call rejected takeoff. I was in contact and instructed to taxi off runway XXR onto taxiway 1 left 2 hold short runway XY. We had stopped on taxiway 1 initially clear of runway XXR while CA made PA to passengers to remain seated and aware of situation. The controller shortly asked after if we would like to return to gate or taxi back for take off and we contacted back that we needed to run checklist and cool off brakes. We then proceeded to hold short runway XY. We ran rejected takeoff checklist in the QRH and came up with a plan with letting tower aware of what was going on. After we continued within standards and proper limitations with a normal and successful departure and arrival.
